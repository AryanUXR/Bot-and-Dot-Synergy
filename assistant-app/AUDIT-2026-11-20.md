# Blue Dots Assistant: UI and accessibility audit

20 Nov 2026 · target WCAG 2.2 AA · build on UAT as of 09:25 IST

**Screens checked:** sign-in, home, chat with all six canvas tabs (Fit check, Brief, Personas, Schema, Map, Voice bot), and the public share page `/p/[id]`. Each was checked on desktop and at 320px phone width.

**Method:**
- axe-core 4.14 automated scan on every screen and tab.
- Contrast computed by hand where axe couldn't decide (text over the animated background).
- A real keyboard pass with Tab, recording where focus lands and whether it shows.
- A structure pass: landmarks, headings, live regions, labels, image alt text and target sizes.
- A 320px reflow check.

**Not done:** a pass with a real screen reader (VoiceOver). That needs a person; the findings below that affect screen readers come from the structure checks.

## Summary

The basics hold up:
- every input has a label;
- every image has alt text, and generated portraits are marked "(generated)";
- the page language is set;
- no target is smaller than 24px;
- the layout reflows at 320px with no sideways scrolling;
- reduced motion is respected;
- the sign-in error is announced.

The gaps cluster in three places:
1. **The map** can't be used without a mouse.
2. **Grey secondary text** is too faint throughout.
3. **Changes that happen in place** aren't announced to screen readers: replies, errors and toasts.

## Status, 20 Nov 2026 (later the same day)

All 10 Tier 1 issues are fixed, and so are parts of Tier 2 (#11 tab semantics with arrow keys, #12 heading levels, #13 share-page landmark and h1, #14 the greeting's language).
- **Re-check:** axe found no violations on sign-in, home, all six canvas tabs or the share page. Contrast passes on every screen, placeholders included (4.66:1). No hidden control stays focusable at 375px.
- **Found during the fixes and also fixed:**
  - the closed sidebar's controls stayed focusable on desktop too;
  - the sidebar's close button stayed invisible while it had focus;
  - the delete buttons appeared only on hover, so a keyboard couldn't reach them.
- **Still to do:** a pass with a real screen reader (VoiceOver), and the five UI notes below.

## UI review (not accessibility)

1. **Persona descriptions are cut off with "…" on the share page.** People choose who to "be" for the call without seeing the whole need. Show two full lines, or reveal the rest when a card is selected.
2. **The home screen asks the same thing twice.** The four mode chips (Ask, Check fit, Shape a brief, Build) and the four suggestion pills below them cover the same four intents, which makes eight controls for four choices. Keep the chips, and make the pills example prompts for the selected mode.
3. **The map legend and filter chips sit on top of the map.** At phone width they can cover the pins nearest the top. Collapse the filters into one "Filter" button under 640px.
4. **Long replies** (about 250 words in comms testing) push the "What to do next" step below the fold. The 150-word rule is in the prompt, but nothing enforces it; check it in the next simulation run.
5. **The Fit tab's new "What to do next" box** works well and reads clearly. Keep that pattern for the Brief and Personas tabs ("Approve and build", "Change a persona").

## What passed

- `lang="en"` and a page title on every page.
- Labels on all inputs; `autocomplete` on the name (`given-name`) and phone (`tel-national`) fields.
- The sign-in error uses `role="alert"`.
- Alt text on every image.
- No target under 24×24px.
- No sideways scrolling at 320px.
- No keyboard traps found.
- The composer and phone field show a focus border.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` stops the orb, the network background and CSS animations.
- The mode chips expose their state with `aria-pressed`.
