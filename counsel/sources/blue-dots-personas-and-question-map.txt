Blue Dots Personas and Question Map
Oct 11, 2026 · @Tushar
Overview
Seven personas are likely to engage with Blue Dots: three adopters and four enablers. Of the questions they ask, docs.bluedotseconomy.org fully answers about a quarter today. Cost, enablers and operating at scale are the largest gaps.
The questions draw on the Learnings Template (adopter questions), neutralised for local discovery rather than a single use case. Questions for enablers are drafted from their side of the work.
Phases:
	•	Setup: deciding to adopt and standing it up.
	•	Pilot: the first district running, building density and outcomes.
	•	Scale: more districts or use cases, interoperability and sustainability.
Coverage status in the mapping:
	•	Answered: the site addresses the question directly.
	•	Partial: the site touches it, but an adopter or enabler would still need to ask.
	•	Missing: the site does not address it.
Personas
Adopters own the deployment and hold convening authority. Enablers are tech enablers who help adopters implement the Blue Dots approach and DPGs in their use case and context. In most scenarios adopters need enablers and will not do it on their own.
Adopter personas are split by role, not by level of government. District, state and central adopters ask largely the same questions. The few that differ for central ministries are tagged central only.
