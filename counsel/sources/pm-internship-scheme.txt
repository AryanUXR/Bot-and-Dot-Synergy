About PM Internship Scheme
The Prime Minister's Internship Scheme (PMIS) is part of the Prime Minister's package of five schemes and initiatives designed to facilitate employment, skilling, and other opportunities for India's youth. PMIS is aimed at youth currently not in full time employment, providing them with paid internship opportunities in leading companies across India. The objective of this scheme is to enhance the employability of Indian youth. The pilot phase of the scheme is currently underway.

Candidate FAQs 
1. General Information 
1. What is the Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PMIS)? 
The Prime Minister Internship Scheme is a Government of India initiative aimed at 
providing internship opportunities to youth in top companies of India. The program 
offers youth exposure to real-life business environments across sectors, helping them 
gain valuable skills and work experience, ultimately enhancing their employability. 
This scheme targets to offer one crore internships to youth over five years. 
2. What is an internship? Why should I enrol myself in an internship program? 
Internship is an arrangement between the intern and the company in which the 
company provides an opportunity to the intern to get training, gain experience and 
skills within the real -life environment of the business or organization. It helps in 
bridging th e gap between academic learning and industry requirements, in turn, 
assisting enhancement of her/his employability. 
3. Through the PM Internship Scheme, what kind of internships will I get? 
Eligible candidates may get internship opportunities in different roles/functions 
across multiple sectors offered by Partner Companies/Organizations through the 
PMIS portal. 
Internship opportunities may be available across different locations/regions in India, 
and candidates can browse and apply based on their preferences such as location 
(state, district), sector, functional role and qualifications. 
The internships may be offered across sectors such as IT and Software Development, 
Banking and Financial Services, Oil, Gas & Energy, Metals & Mining, FMCG (Fast -
Moving Consumer Goods), Telecom, Infrastructure & Construction, Retail & 
Consumer Durables, Ce ment & Building Materials, Automotive, Pharmaceutical, 
Aviation & Defence, Manufacturing & Industrial, Chemical, Media, Entertainment & 
Education, Agriculture and Allied, Consulting Services, Textile Manufacturing, Gems 
and Jewellery, Travel & Hospitality and Healthcare, etc. 
The scheme is designed to support participation of youth from diverse backgrounds, 
and the portal shortlisting approach considers broader representation across the 
applicant base. This enables youth from both rural and urban areas, and 
students/candidates from different educational backgrounds, to explore internship 
opportunities with top companies through PMIS. 
4. What kind of companies will I get to intern with? 
Selected candidates will have the opportunity to intern with the top 
companies/organizations from across India. Interning with these industry -leading 
companies/organizations will allow you to gain valuable experience, acquire new 
skills, and build important professional networks. Eligible partner company may 
provide such internship opportunities in its own company, or it may tie up with 
Companies/MSMEs in its forward and backward supply chain (e.g. Network Entities 
/ Subsidiaries / Suppliers / Vendors etc.), or in other Companies/Institutions in its 
Group. 
5. What is the duration of the internship? 
Internships under the PM Internship Scheme will be for a duration of either 6 months 
or 9 months, depending on nature of internship, sector and company requirement. It 
is clarified that internship duration for the existing interns of the earlier pilot rounds 
will remain 12 months. 
6. Will I be sitting in a classroom during my internship? 
At least half of the internship period must be spent in the actual working experience/ 
job environment, and not in the classroom. While a certain part of the internship may 
include relevant training, a significant duration (at least half) will focus on real-world 
experience in professional setups. 
7. Will I get a job after my internship? 
The PM Internship Scheme provides a valuable opportunity to learn and enhance your 
skills by working with Partner Companies/Organizations. While the scheme does not 
guarantee a job upon completion, the knowledge, practical experience, and 
professional networks you develop during the internship will significantly enhance 
your career prospects. 
2. Eligibility Criteria 
1. Can I apply for the PM Internship Scheme? 
To apply for the PM Internship Scheme, you must meet the following criteria: 
• You must be an Indian citizen. 
• You must be aged between 18-25 years (as on the date of submission of 
application). 
• You must not be employed full-time. 
• You must not be engaged in full-time education. However, candidates enrolled 
in online or distance learning programmes are eligible, and candidates 
enrolled in the final year of full-time graduation or post -graduation are also 
eligible to apply. 
• You must have passed High School/Secondary School, Higher Secondary 
School, possess a certificate from an ITI, hold a diploma from a Polytechnic 
Institute, or be an graduate (e.g., BA, B.Sc., B.Com, BCA, BBA, B.Pharma, 
B.E./B.Tech etc.) or a postgraduate (e.g., MA, M.Sc., M.Com, MCA, M.Tech 
etc.) as per the scheme guidelines. 
• You may also check your eligibility through the PMIS portal 
(www.pminternship.mca.gov.in) or through the PMIS mobile application 
2. Can Foreign Nationals apply for the PM Internship Scheme? 
No, Foreign Nationals are not eligible to apply for the PM Internship Scheme. 
3. Can I apply for an internship opportunity if I am enrolled in an online/distance/ 
correspondence course? 
Yes. Candidates enrolled in online/distance/correspondence learning programmes 
are eligible to apply. 
4. Is there any provision for reservation to promote diversity and inclusion? 
While the PM Internship Scheme does not have formal reservation provisions, the 
portal is designed to promote diversity and social inclusion. The algorithm ensures 
fair representation of all segments of the population, including SCs, STs, OBCs, and 
PwDs. 
The shortlisting process emphasizes diversity and inclusion, allowing companies to 
select candidates based on their own criteria, while the portal ensures that candidates 
from diverse backgrounds are considered for internships. This approach helps 
promote social inclusivity in the program. 
5. I am ineligible to apply because of my age. Can you please relax the age criteria? 
As of now, the age criteria for the Prime Minister Internship Scheme are 18-25 years 
(as on the date of submission of application). If there is any relaxation in the age 
criteria in the future, the same will be communicated to all stakeholders. 
3. Ineligibility Conditions 
1. Who is not eligible for the PM Internship scheme? 
You are not eligible to apply for the PM Internship Scheme if: 
• You are below 18 years or above 25 years of age (as on the date of submission 
of application). 
• You are currently engaged in full-time employment. 
• You are currently engaged in full -time education (except those enrolled in 
online/distance learning programmes and those in the final year of full -time 
graduation or post-graduation, as permitted). 
• You have graduated or post graduated from specific institutions like IITs, IIMs, 
National Law Universities, IISER, NIDs, IIITs, and IISc. 
• You hold qualifications such as CA, CMA, CS, MBBS, BDS, MD, MS, MBA 
(or equivalent), MPhil or PhD. 
• You are undergoing any skill, apprenticeship, internship, or training 
programme under a Central or State Government scheme. 
• You have completed apprenticeship or training under NATS or NAPS at any 
point. 
• The income of any of your family members (self, parents, or spouse) exceeds 
₹12 lakh in the preceding financial year , as on the date of submission of 
application. 
• Any member of your family (self, parents, or spouse) is a permanent/regular 
government employee (excluding contractual employees). “Government” 
includes Central and State Governments, Union Territory administrations, 
Central and State PSUs, statutory organizations, and local bodies etc. 
2. Will having a master’s degree help me while applying for the PM Internship 
Scheme? 
Candidates with eligible post -graduate degrees such as MA, M.Sc., M.Com, MCA, 
M.Tech etc. may apply, subject to meeting the other eligibility conditions. However, 
candidates having qualifications such as MBA (or equivalent), MPhil or PhD cannot 
apply for an internship opportunity under the PM Internship Scheme. 
4. Other Questions 
1. How will the Ministry recognize interns for their performance? 
Interns may be recognized by the respective company as per its policies, based on their 
performance. 
2. How would my queries or complaints be resolved? 
Complaints/Grievances: You can raise and track complaints through the 
“Complaints” section on the PMIS portal. This section allows you to report issues, 
submit supporting information (if any), and monitor progress until final resolution. 
How to raise a complaint: 
1) Open the Complaints section: From the left-side menu, click “Complaints”. The 
Complaints page will display a summary of complaint statuses at the top. 
2) File a new complaint: Click the option to “File a Complaint” / “Raise a new 
issue”. 
3) Enter complaint details (mandatory fields) 
a. Main Category: Select the nature of the issue: 
i. DBT Related 
ii. Internship Related 
iii. Portal Related 
b. Subcategory (mandatory): Select the relevant option. 
c. Sub-subcategory (mandatory): Select the relevant option. 
d. Description (mandatory): Clearly describe the issue with key details 
(what happened, when it happened, and any relevant reference 
information). 
4) Upload supporting documents. Providing supporting evidence helps in 
quicker and more accurate resolution. 
5) Submit the complaint: Click “Submit Complaint”. After submission, track 
progress using the status tiles available on the Complaints page. 
Queries/General Help: For general queries and guidance, candidates may: 
• Refer to the Candidate User Manuals and Guidance Videos available on the 
PMIS portal. 
• Use the chatbot available on the PMIS portal for quick assistance. 
• Refer to the Dos and Don’ts/instructions available on the portal. 
• Contact the multilingual Helpline call centre at 1800 -11-6090 for additional 
support. 
3. Is there a guarantee of employment after completing the internship? 
No, the PMIS does not guarantee employment after completing the internship. 
However, companies may choose to offer employment to interns who demonstrate 
exceptional performance during their tenure. It is important to note that an internship 
offer does not create any contractual or legal employer -employee relationship 
between the Ministry, the company and the intern, and the internship should not be 
construed as an offer or promise of future employment by either the company or the 
Ministry. 
5. Financial Assistance 
1. Will I get financial assistance for doing my internship? 
Yes. After joining the internship, each intern will receive monthly assistance of ₹9,000 
for the entire duration of the internship. In addition, some Partner Companies may 
offer extra financial or non-financial benefits (if any). These benefits are displayed on 
the portal/mobile app as icons on the internship opportunity tiles on the main/home 
page and are also listed in the detailed internship description under the “Additional 
Benefits” section (and may also be reiterated in the offer details shared by the 
company) 
Of this amount: 
• Company share: ₹900 per month (10%) paid by the Partner Company (from 
CSR funds or own funds). This may be linked to attendance and adherence to 
company policies. 
• Government share: Up to ₹8,100 per month paid via DBT to the intern’s 
Aadhaar-seeded bank account after the company uploads payment details on 
the portal. The Government amount is pro -rated based on the company’s 
payment. 
One-time incidental support: A one-time incidental grant of ₹6,000 is provided by the 
Government via DBT in two instalments of ₹3,000 each. 
• 1st instalment: initiated after joining is verified on the portal. 
• 2nd instalment: after completion of 90 days as active intern (Including 
sabbatical period if rejoined), minimum 2 monthly allowance payment done & 
uploaded by the company and at the time of payment status must be “Joining 
verified by HR”. 
The government will provide each Intern with the Life and Personal Accidental 
Insurances offering coverage and benefits equivalent to those under of Pradhan 
Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) and Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima 
Yojana (PMSBY). 
2. How can I apply for insurance? 
Interns need not apply for insurance separately. The Ministry of Corporate Affairs 
(MCA) will automatically provide Life and Accidental insurance coverage through a 
Group Insurance arrangement for all eligible interns under the Scheme. 
3. What is the eligibility of interns to receive initial DBT and the monthly 
internship allowance? 
Only those interns who have joined the internship, whose joining is verified by HR, 
and who have an Aadhaar seeded bank account are eligible to receive the incidental 
grant (DBT) and the monthly assistance. 
Also, as per the rolling mechanism: 
• If your status is “ Joining verified by HR ” during the month, you are eligible 
to receive the monthly allowance from the company , and the Government 
share is released after the company uploads payment. 
• If your status is Dropout, Terminated, or On Sabbatical, you are ineligible for 
that month. 
4. Will I get travel allowances or other support during the duration of the 
internship? 
Partner Companies may consider providing additional financial assistance or any 
other facilities such as transportation support, meal benefits etc. as per their discretion. 
The internships that you opt for may or may not have any other benefits attached to 
them. 
5. Will the insurance cover continue after the internship? 
The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) provides Life and Accidental insurance 
coverage through a Group Insurance arrangement only for the duration of the 
internship. After the internship ends, the Scheme ‑linked insurance coverage will 
cease. For any continuation of coverage beyond the internship period, interns may 
refer to the applicable policy terms and consult the insurer or bank channel directly. 
6. Will I receive financial assistance during the sabbatical (break) period? 
No. Financial assistance will not be provided during the sabbatical (break) period. 
7. Will I get any financial assistance to purchase clothes for my internship? 
No specific financial assistance shall be provided for the purchase of clothes. 
However, the Government provides a one-time incidental grant of ₹6,000 (in two 
tranches of ₹3,000) through DBT after joining and after completion of 3 months of 
internship. 
8. What is an Aadhaar seeded bank account? 
An Aadhaar seeded bank account is a bank account in which your Aadhaar number 
is linked (seeded) with the account. Aadhaar seeding is necessary to receive scheme 
benefits such as Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT), including the monthly assistance and 
other eligible payments under PMIS. You can refer to the Aadhaar seeding process 
document here: https://dbtbharat.gov.in/data/aadhaar-uidai/Aadhaar-seeding-process.pdf 
How to check Aadhaar seeding status (on PMIS portal): 
• Go to “My Profile” and click “Check Aadhaar Seeding Status”. 
• The portal will display your current Aadhaar seeding status with your bank 
account. 
How to complete Aadhaar seeding via the PMIS portal (redirect to NPCI): 
1) Go to “My Profile” and click “Click here for Aadhaar Seeding”. 
2) You will be redirected to the NPCI BASE (Bharat Aadhaar Seeding Enabler) 
website (external page). 
3) On the NPCI page: 
• From the left menu, select Aadhaar Seeding/Deseeding. 
• Enter your Aadhaar number. 
• Under “Request for Aadhaar”, select “Seeding”. 
• Select your bank from the dropdown. 
• Enter your account number and re-enter it in “Confirm Account Number”. 
4) Provide consent and complete verification: 
• Tick the consent checkbox. 
• Enter the captcha exactly as shown on the screen. 
5) Click Submit/Continue to submit your seeding request on NPCI. 
6) Return to PMIS → “My Profile” and click “Check Aadhaar Seeding Status” to 
confirm whether your status is updated. 
9. Will I receive the monthly internship allowance if I discontinue the internship 
midway? 
If you withdraw/dropout from the internship, your status will be updated on the 
portal, and you will be ineligible to receive the monthly assistance for the month(s) 
when your status is Dropout/Terminated/On Sabbatical, as applicable. Also, if you 
discontinue the internship before completion, you will not receive the internship 
completion certificate . However, a Participation Certificate may be issued (upon 
request) if you have completed at least half of the prescribed internship duration 
6. Registration Process 
1. How can I view the information on the portal in my language? 
The Prime Minister Internship Scheme portal is accessible in multiple languages, 
including Hindi, English, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, 
Oriya, Punjabi, Telugu, and Tamil. You can change the language from the dropdown 
menu in the top-right corner of the navigation bar of the portal. 
2. The portal is not working properly. How can I apply? 
If the portal is not loading or is working slowly, please try the following 
troubleshooting steps: 
• Refresh the page and try again after a few minutes. 
• Check your internet connection (switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, if 
possible). 
• Close and reopen the browser or restart your device. 
• Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try logging in again. 
• Try using an Incognito/Private window, or use a different browser (e.g., 
Chrome/Edge/Firefox). 
• Update your browser to the latest version and retry (older versions may not 
support all portal features). 
• Disable browser extensions (especially ad-blockers) and try again. 
• Try accessing the portal from a different device (mobile/desktop) to rule out 
device-specific issues. 
If the problem persists and you are unable to access the portal, please contact the help 
centre at the Helpline number 1800-11-6090. 
3. I have not received OTP on my SMS / Email. What should I do? 
If you have not received the OTP on Email: 
• Check your Inbox and Spam/Junk folder. 
• If you find the OTP in Spam/Junk, use the latest OTP received. 
• If the OTP has expired or is not working, click “Resend OTP” on the portal and 
try again. 
If you have not received the OTP on SMS (mobile): 
• Ensure your phone has network coverage and is able to receive SMS (check 
signal, DND/SMS blocking settings, and available storage). 
• Restart your phone and try “Resend OTP” on the portal. 
• Check that the mobile number entered/registered is correct and active. 
If you still do not receive the OTP (Email or SMS): 
• If you are unable to access the portal, contact the Helpline number 1800 -11-
6090. 
4. I have received multiple OTPs, what should I do? 
If you have received multiple OTPs, use the latest OTP received. If you are still not 
able to login, you can raise a grievance on the portal or call the help centre at our 
Helpline number 1800-11-6090 to raise a ticket. 
5. Why am I unable to apply for the internship opportunities on the portal? 
Internships are posted on the portal on a rolling basis and you can browse and apply 
anytime as long as the internship application window is open for that opportunity. If 
you are unable to apply, it may be because: 
• you already have the maximum number of active applications, or 
• you already have 2 offers that need action 
• you have already accepted an offer (other applications/offers get blocked), or 
• your account has a temporary restriction/ban due to repeated offer 
lapses/rejections/DNR (Did Not Report). 
• If you’re still unable to apply, you can raise a complaint via the “Complaints” 
section present in the left navigation pane on the PMIS Portal or the Helpline 
number 1800-11-6090 to raise a ticket. 
6. I'm within the eligible age range, but the portal says I'm out of the age range. 
What should I do? 
The age is fetched from your Aadhaar. Please verify if your Aadhaar details are 
correct. In case of any discrepancy, please visit the nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre 
for updating the date of birth. Also, check UIDAI website for necessary documents 
required for updating the date of birth. 
7. I need assistance with filling out the registration details. What should I do? 
A detailed user manual and video tutorial is available on the portal under the 
"Manual" section and “Guidance Videos” section in the navigation menu [(Homepage 
>> Manuals >> User Manual (candidate)] or [(Homepage >> Guidance Videos >> 
Videos (Candidates)]. They provide a step -by-step guide for completing the 
registration and profile creation process. If you need any further assistance, you may 
call the help centre at our Helpline number 1800-11-6090. 
You can also access the chatbot present on the bottom left of the PMIS Portal for any 
guidance related to registration details. 
8. I am not able to set my password. What should I do? 
Your initial login OTP is sent via SMS to your registered mobile number. Ensure you 
create the password correctly, as it is case -sensitive. The new password must have a 
minimum of 8 characters and include one uppercase letter (A-Z), one lowercase letter 
(a-z), one special character (!,@,#,$,%,&,*), and one numeric digit (0 -9). Examples of 
valid passwords: Riya@2002, Abcdef123#. 
9. What happens if I enter the wrong password multiple times during login? 
If you enter an incorrect password three times during login attempts in one active 
session, your account will be temporarily locked for 15 minutes. Once this 15 -minute 
waiting period is over, you will need to reset your account and password in order to 
regain access. 
10. Why am I unable to upload my educational and skill qualification certificates 
while registering on the portal? 
Ensure that the certificates are in PDF format and the file size does not exceed 7 MB. 
You can use any tools or applications to create/compress PDF to meet the size 
requirement. 
11. I do not have a DigiLocker Account. How can I apply? 
You can choose to complete your e-KYC through Aadhaar. If you want to complete 
the e-KYC through DigiLocker account, you can download the DigiLocker app from 
Play Store (android)/App Store (iOS). 
12. What is the application fee for the registration process 
There is no registration or application fee for the PM Internship Scheme process. The 
entire process is free of charge. 
13. Which documents will I require for registering on the PM Internship Scheme 
portal? 
Candidates must register on the portal through Aadhaar authentication/e -KYC and 
provide the required personal, educational and contact details as specified on the 
portal. Proof of educational qualifications and certifications (if any) would have to be 
uploaded by the candidates as required. Additionally, candidates must submit a self-
declaration on the portal confirming that they meet eligibility requirements and that 
none of the ineligibility conditions apply 
14. My mobile is updated in my Aadhaar number, but I cannot use it to link my 
DigiLocker account with Aadhaar. It gives an error message – Mobile number is 
not linked with Aadhaar? 
For linking your DigiLocker account with Aadhaar, your mobile number must be 
linked with your Aadhaar number. Please make sure this is actually the case. To verify 
this, visit https://eaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/ and download your eAadhaar. For other 
issues with DigiLocker, please refer to DigiLocker FAQs or raise a ticket with 
DigiLocker support. 
15. Can I edit my details after completing the profile creation? 
Yes, you can edit your details are completing the profile creation. Candidates are 
advised to carefully enter their details while completing the profile to avoid any issues 
later. 
16. How can I view my Candidate ID? 
Candidate ID is your unique identifier on the PMIS portal. To view it: 
• Log in to the PMIS portal using your registered credentials. 
• After login, you will land on your Candidate Dashboard/Home screen. 
• Your Candidate ID is displayed on the screen near your profile details 
(typically at the top-left area, just above/near your name). 
Candidates are advised to keep this information confidential and accessible for future 
communications. 
17. How to fill in the qualification details? 
The candidates must enter the qualification details in order of completion. For 
example, start with class 10, followed by class 12 and then graduation/post -
graduation (as applicable). Make sure the order is correct. 
18. How can I seek help if I get stuck on the PMIS portal at any step? 
If you need assistance at any step on the PMIS portal, you can use the available help 
options below: 
• Use the “Need Help” option available on the portal screen (bottom -right) for 
quick guidance and support. 
• Refer to the Candidate User Manuals and Guidance Videos available on the 
portal (Homepage >> Manuals and Homepage >> Guidance Videos). These 
provide step -by-step instructions for registration, profile creation, and other 
key actions. 
• If you need to report an issue or raise a complaint (once you are able to log in), 
go to the Complaints section and use “File a Complaint” to submit the issue 
and track its status. 
• If you are unable to resolve the issue through the portal tools, contact the help 
centre at the Helpline number 1800-11-6090 for assistance and to log a ticket. 
• You can also raise a request/ticket via Home >> Support >> Raise Request 
(when the portal is accessible). 
7. Rolling Application & Offer Process 
1. When can I register and apply for internship opportunities? 
Participating companies may post internship opportunities on the PMIS portal at any 
time. Internship listings are made available based on when companies choose to 
upload them, and each opportunity will have its own application start and end date. 
Candidates can search, view, and apply only while the application window for a 
specific opportunity is open. 
2. How many internships can I apply for at a time? 
You can have a maximum of 3 active applications at any time. If one of your 
applications is not shortlisted or rejected, you may apply to more internships, but you 
can only have up to 3 active applications at a time. 
3. What does “Active application” mean? 
An “active application” is any internship application that is still in progress on the 
PMIS portal i.e., it has not reached a final closure stage yet. As soon as you apply for 
an internship opportunity, that application becomes “active” and continues to remain 
active while it is moving through the process. 
An application is treated as active when its status is any of the following (examples): 
• Applied 
• Waitlisted 
• Selected / Offer received 
• Offer Accepted 
• Joined (until the internship lifecycle progresses further as per portal process) 
You can have a maximum of 3 active applications at any time. If one of your 
applications is not shortlisted or is rejected, you may apply to more internships, but 
you can still have only up to 3 active applications at a time. 
An application typically stops being counted as active when it is no longer in progress, 
for example: 
• If the company marks it as not considered / rejected. 
• If you reject an offer or the offer lapses due to no action within the timeline, 
that offer / application is no longer in progress. 
• If the portal allows you to withdraw an application and you withdraw it, it will 
no longer count as active (as per portal functionality). 
4. How many offers can I receive and accept? 
You can receive up to 2 offers at any given time, but you can accept only 1 offer. 
5. How much time do I have to accept or reject an offer? What happens if I do 
nothing? 
You have 14 days to take action (Accept/Reject) on an offer. If you do not accept or 
reject the offer, it will automatically lapse on the 15th day. 
6. What happens after I accept an offer? Can I still apply to other internships? 
Once you accept an offer, you cannot apply to more internships (other 
applications/offers will be blocked). 
7. If I get 2 offers, can I change my decision after accepting one? 
If you receive 2 offers and have accepted one, you may withdraw the accepted offer 
and accept the other offer only within 15 days from your acceptance date. 
8. By when do I need to join after accepting an offer? 
You must join on or before the reporting date mentioned in the offer letter. If you do 
not join by the reporting date, you get a 14-day grace period to join. If you still do not 
join within the grace period, the system will mark you as Did Not Report (DNR) 
automatically on the 15th day. 
9. Can my joining date be extended? 
Your supervisor can approve an extension of up to 1 month from the original 
reporting date. If you want to join later, you must request the extension and join on 
the extended date. 
10. If I drop out of an internship, can I apply again? 
An intern who drops out is permitted to apply afresh for an internship. Such a 
candidate is allowed a maximum of one additional chance to participate and join as 
an intern in the pilot project. If the candidate drops out again, she/he will not be 
permitted to participate again. 
11. If I am terminated by the company, can I participate again? 
An intern whose internship has been terminated by the participating 
company/organization will not be permitted to participate in the pilot project again. 
12. Are there any penalties if I dropout or repeatedly reject/lapse offers or do not 
report after acceptance? 
If you have dropped out, you will not be allowed to participate in PMIS for 6 months. 
Any candidate who drops out is allowed only one more opportunity to join an 
internship under PMIS. If they drop out again (second dropout), they are permanently 
ineligible to participate in the Scheme. 
If you have 2 consecutive instances of Offer Rejected / Offer Lapsed / Did Not Report 
(DNR), you will be banned for 3 months from applying on PMIS. If you have 2 total 
(cumulative) instances of Offer Rejected / Offer Lapsed / Did Not Report (DNR), you 
will be banned for 1 year from applying on PMIS. 
13. When will I receive the one-time support (₹6,000) and what are the conditions? 
The one-time support is paid by the Government in two instalments of ₹3,000 each. 
The 1st instalment is initiated when Joining is verified by HR. The 2nd instalment is 
paid only if the company has paid minimum 2 months allowance and you have 90 
active days (90 days joining verified by HR). 
14. Will my internship end date change if I take a sabbatical (emergency break)? 
You can take a sabbatical for genuine emergency reasons only. You can take a break 
multiple times, but all sabbaticals combined cannot be more than 2 months. Every 
time you take a sabbatical, your internship end date gets extended by the same 
number of days you were on break. 
15. Can I extend the internship beyond the prescribed duration? 
No, the internship duration is fixed as per the internship opportunity/offer. 
Extensions are not permitted. However, if you take an approved sabbatical (break), 
your internship end date will be extended by the sabbatical period. 
16. Can I take leaves during my internship? 
Yes, you can take leaves in accordance with the company's policies. 
17. Will I get holidays? 
Yes, selected candidates will be eligible for holidays as per their company’s holiday 
list. 
18. In case of a medical or any other emergency, what do I do? 
In case of a medical emergency, the demise of a family member, or such extenuating 
and emergency circumstances, a sabbatical of up to two months may be granted in 
accordance with company policies and/or directives issued from time to time. 
Internship duration will be extended to cover the sabbatical period and no financial 
assistance shall be provided during the sabbatical period. 
19. How do I request a sabbatical (break) on the portal? 
You must raise a request for sabbatical by specifying the dates and reasons through 
the “Sabbatical Request” option/tile on the portal. The request must be approved by 
the Supervisor and subsequently verified/approved by HR, as applicable on the 
portal. Approval is mandatory before the sabbatical is granted. 
20. Can I rejoin the internship after a sabbatical (break)? 
Yes, you may rejoin and complete the remaining duration of the internship, provided 
the total sabbatical period does not exceed two months. Your internship end date will 
be extended by the number of days you were on sabbatical. 
21. What happens if the sabbatical (break) period is more than two months? 
Sabbatical beyond two months is not permitted. In such a case, you may be required 
to discontinue the internship as per portal/company process. 
22. Will I receive financial assistance during the sabbatical (break) period?* 
No. Financial assistance will not be provided during the sabbatical (break) period. 
23. What happens if I need to leave the internship (drop out) before completion? 
If you want to drop out, you must raise a dropout request through the portal using 
the “Dropout Request” option/tile. The request is confirmed only after approval as 
per portal/company workflow. Once marked as dropout, you will not be eligible for 
the completion certificate. 
24. Will there be any training provided before the internship starts? 
Training is generally provided after joining the internship as per company 
requirements. The company may provide relevant training (theoretical and practical) 
during the internship. 
25. How can I view the training schedule assigned by the company? 
You can view the training schedule and related details/documents (where made 
available) in the Training Schedule / Quarterly Training Schedule option/tile under 
the “My Internship” section on the portal. 
26. Are there any performance evaluations during the internship? 
Yes, interns may be evaluated periodically (including quarterly, where applicable) 
based on performance, adherence to company policies and skill development. 
Feedback may be provided by the Supervisor and verified by HR as per 
portal/company process. 
27. How can I check if I have received the one -time support ( ₹6,000) from the 
Government? 
How to check the status on the PMIS portal: 
1) Log in to the PMIS portal using your registered credentials. 
2) From your Candidate Dashboard, go to the “My Internship” section. 
3) Open the DBT / one-time support / payment-related tile (where available) to view 
the current status and transaction details of the support amount. 
When and how the one -time support is paid: The one -time support of ₹6,000 is paid 
by the Government through DBT in two instalments of ₹3,000 each. 
• 1st instalment: initiated when your joining is verified by HR on the portal. 
• 2nd instalment: paid after completion of 3 months of internship, subject to 
rolling conditions (including the company having paid minimum 2 months 
allowance and you having 90 active days with joining verified by HR). 
28. How will I check if I have received my monthly internship allowance? 
You can check the status of monthly allowance through the relevant 
allowance/payment option/tile in the “My Internship” section. The payment details 
will be displayed as per portal view. 
29. What kind of projects will I work on as an intern? 
Interns may work on a variety of projects depending on the company’s needs and the 
intern’s skills. Roles and responsibilities are specified in the internship details and 
offer letter. Candidates are advised to carefully read the description. 
30. What kind of clothes should I wear during my internship? 
Dress code requirements may vary depending on the company. Interns are expected 
to dress professionally and adhere to the company’s dress code policies. 
31. Will I receive a certificate after completing the internship? 
Yes, interns who successfully complete the internship will receive a Certificate of 
Completion issued by the Partner Company through the portal, as applicable. Where 
an intern exits early (drops out), a Participation Certificate may be issued upon 
request, provided the intern has completed at least half of the prescribed internship 
duration. 
8. Grievance (Complaints) Redressal Mechanism 
1. Who can raise a grievance under the PM Internship Scheme? 
Candidates (not registered on the PMIS portal) can raise grievances only for 
technical/portal issues, such as login problems, OTP not received, KYC errors, page 
loading issues, or document upload errors. 
Registered candidates can raise grievances under all available categories , which 
include DBT-related issues (one-time grant and monthly assistance), Internship-
related issues (joining verification, HR concerns, supervisor issues, sabbatical, 
dropout/termination, workplace concerns), and Portal-related issues (technical 
problems). 
2. How can Candidates raise Grievance (Complaints) under the PM Internship 
Scheme? 
To raise a grievance, registered candidates should: 
1. Log in to the PMIS Portal. 
2. Open the “Complaints” section from the left-side menu. 
3. Click “Complaints / Raise a New Issue.” 
4. Select the Main Category, Subcategory, and Sub-subcategory. 
5. Enter a clear description and upload relevant supporting documents. 
6. Submit the grievance and track its progress through the status tiles on the 
Complaints page. 
Candidates may also refer to the User Manuals, Guidance Videos, Chatbot, or 
Helpline for assistance. 
3. What to attach for faster resolution of Grievance (Complaints)? 
• Candidate ID / Internship ID, company name & location 
• Screenshots (error messages, offer/joining page, stipend tile, bank seeding 
status) 
• Dates & contact attempts (emails/calls with HR/Supervisor) 
• Payment proof, or Aadhaar seeding confirmation (if candidate side) 
4. Where to track Grievance (Complaints) status & get help? 
• Track in Complaints → status tiles (portal). 
• User Manuals and Guidance Videos for step-by-step help. 
• Chatbot for quick answers. 
• Helpline: 1800-11-6090 (multilingual). 
5. When not to raise a Grievance (Complaints)? 
• Application rejected / not shortlisted (no policy right to selection). 
• Preference mismatch (location/role/batch not as desired). 
Portal downtime of a few minutes (try refreshing by clicking 
ctrl+shift+R/incognito/another browser per troubleshooting guidance).

1. Criteria For Participation 
1. What are the eligibility criteria for companies to participate in the PM 
Internship Scheme (PMIS)? 
Companies can participate under Category A or Category B as per Section 5.3 of the 
Guidelines. 
Category A – Automatically Eligible / CSR-Based Companies 
The following are eligible: 
1. Existing PMIS Pilot Participants 
Companies that participated in: 
• Round 1 and 
• Round 2 
are automatically eligible for the Pilot Project Extension (till Dec 2026). 
2. Companies with CSR expenditure in each of the last 3 financial years 
Companies that have incurred CSR expenditure in: 
• FY 2024–25 
• FY 2023–24 
• FY 2022–23 
AND meet any one of the following criteria (subject to MCA approval): 
• Among the Top 2000 companies by average CSR spend 
• Annual turnover > ₹1,000 crore (preceding FY) 
• Net worth > ₹500 crore (preceding FY) 
• Companies from under-represented sectors 
MCA will review eligibility and documentation before approval. 
Category B – Special Organizations (Approval Required) 
The following may also participate: 
• Professional Associations (ICAI, ICSI, ICMAI) 
• Selected MSMEs (through Sectoral Associations) 
• Global Capability Centres (GCCs) 
• Companies in SEZs 
• State/UT-nominated companies 
• Statutory bodies such as AAI, SCI, etc. 
A separate operational SOP for these entities will be issued by MCA. 
2. My company is not part of the Top 2000 CSR list. Can we still participate? 
Yes. You may still join PMIS through the New Partner Company (Category A) 
onboarding pathway, provided that: 
Mandatory Requirement 
Your company has CSR expenditure recorded in: 
• FY 2024–25 
• FY 2023–24 
• FY 2022–23 
AND meets at least one of the following financial/sector conditions: 
• Annual turnover > ₹1,000 crore 
• Net worth > ₹500 crore 
• Operates in an under-represented sector 
AND meets operational commitments: 
• Ability to post a minimum of 50 internship opportunities per financial year 
• Ability to set up a PMIS Cell (Admin, Nodal Officer, SPOC) 
After evaluation, MCA may approve your onboarding. 
3. My company cannot directly host interns. Can we still participate? 
Yes. As per Guideline 5.3.2, companies may offer internship seats through: 
1. Forward and backward supply chain 
• Vendors 
• Suppliers 
• Customers 
• MSMEs within the ecosystem 
2. Group Companies / Network Companies 
• Subsidiaries 
• Sister companies 
• Joint ventures 
• Group entities 
Each participating entity must: 
• Have its own login 
• Declare its own workforce 
• Adhere to PMIS norms individually 
This helps expand opportunities across Tier 2/3/4 regions using the company’s 
ecosystem. 
4. What is the maximum number of internships a company can post? 
Under Section 5.3.3, companies must follow the 15% ceiling rule: 
Maximum Capacity = 15% of total workforce 
Workforce includes: 
• Regular employees 
• Contractual staff on payroll 
For network entities and subsidiaries: 
• The 15% cap applies individually to each entity 
• Workforce numbers must be self-declared on the PMIS Portal 
Minimum Requirement 
Each Partner Company must commit to: 
• At least 50 internship opportunities per financial year 
5. What documents must new companies submit to join PMIS? 
During onboarding (as per Round 3 SOP), companies must submit, on the PMIS 
Portal: 
A. Internship Requirements (Per internship role): 
• Company Name 
• Location (State, District) 
• Qualification requirements 
• Role/Title 
• Number of internship opportunities 
• Additional financial assistance (Yes/No) 
• Benefits (accommodation, food, transport, etc.) 
B. Internship Plan 
• Motivation to participate 
• Detailed role descriptions 
• Skills interns will gain 
• Training & Induction Plan 
• Mentoring & supervision framework 
• Employability enhancement steps 
C. PMIS Cell Details 
Mandatory: 
• Company Admin 
• Nodal Officer 
• PMIS SPOC (Director/CHRO Level) 
Optional: 
• Additional HR/CSR/Finance/IT team members 
D. Preference Advantage (Not mandatory but beneficial) 
Preference is given to companies offering: 
• Additional financial support 
• Accommodation for interns 
• Opportunities for women and PwD candidates 
• Internships in rural/Tier 2/3/4 areas 
• Internships in NE, Hilly regions, J&K 
• Roles in under-represented / sunrise sectors 
6. What happens after we submit an Expression of Interest (EOI)? 
Once the EOI is submitted via the PMIS Portal: 
Step 1: Eligibility Check by MCA 
MCA reviews: 
• CSR data 
• Sector representation 
• Documentation completeness 
• Internship preparedness 
Step 2: Approval 
Approved companies proceed to login creation. 
Step 3: Login Activation 
The Portal auto-generates: 
• Company Admin credentials 
• Email with login details 
Step 4: Company Setup 
Admin must: 
• Log in 
• Reset password 
• Complete Company Profile 
• Create Nodal Officer & HR Users 
You are then ready to post internship opportunities. 
7. Is there a fixed onboarding calendar for new companies? 
Yes — PMIS follows a Monthly Onboarding Cycle for Round 3: 
1st–15th of every month: EOI & onboarding request window open 
16th–30th/31st: 
• Eligibility verification 
• MCA approvals 
• Account creation by BISAG-N 
• Orientation & training 
This ensures: 
• Predictable timelines 
• Administrative efficiency 
• Quality control in onboarding 
8. Is participation in PMIS mandatory for eligible companies? 
No. Participation is voluntary for all companies, including top CSR spenders. 
However, PMIS offers: 
• Contribution to workforce development and Viksit Bharat Mission 
• Industry recognition through MCA awards 
• Support for regional/MSME ecosystem development 
• National visibility 
2. Portal Questions 
9. What user types are allowed to access the portal from the industry/company 
side? What can each role do? 
Important: Only HR can create internship postings. Camp postings require 
Admin/Nodal approval to go live. Only Admin can edit the Company Landing 
Page. 
10. What is the role of a Nodal Officer? 
The Nodal Officer is the operational owner of PMIS within the company. They: 
• Create HR users and manage their access 
• Approve/Reject Camp Mobilisation postings (as checker; may be delegated by 
Admin) 
• Handle grievances (L2) if HR doesn’t respond within 7 days (internship issues) or 
4 days (DBT issues) 
• Use Status Reversal (e.g., Offer Lapsed → Selected by Company; Eligibility 
reversal for DBT) 
• Ensure compliance with posting rules (no menial roles, correct geo-tagging, QCS 
correctness) 
• Oversee internship lifecycle KPIs and adherence to 15% cap and monthly stipend 
uploads 
11. What is the recommended number of interns assigned per HR? 
There’s no fixed cap. Companies may decide based on internal bandwidth and 
governance. 
Best practice: Keep HR load manageable to ensure timely actions (shortlist within 45 
days, offer acceptance follow-ups, joining verification, stipend upload by 5th, QPR 
approvals, grievance TATs). 
12. How can a company view and manage grievances raised by candidates? 
Where to view: Grievance section in the PMIS portal (visible to HR & Nodal Officer). 
Workflow & Turnaround Times (TATs) 
• Internship-related grievances (work environment, menial work, safety, training, 
certificate delays, selection/contract issues): 
• Level 1 – HR: Respond within 7 days 
• Level 2 – Nodal Officer: If HR doesn’t respond, 3 days 
• If still unresolved → escalates to ROC/Regional PMIS Cell 
• DBT/stipend-related grievances (company share, incorrect amount, DBT not 
received): 
• HR: 4 days 
• If unresolved → ROC/Regional PMIS Cell: 7 days 
• If unresolved → PMU: 4 days 
• If unresolved → MCA: Final escalation 
• One-time grant grievances: handled by PMU (TAT 8 days) 
Good practice: Record actions taken, attach evidence where relevant, and 
proactively call candidates for quick resolution. 
13. Can HR users see the number of supervisors created for their company? 
Yes. HR can view supervisor counts in the Users View on the dashboard and 
manage (create/edit/delete) supervisors as needed. 
14. I have not received my login credentials as a Nodal Officer. How can I access 
my account? 
• Verify with Admin that your email & mobile are correct in PMIS 
• Check spam/junk and ensure a stable internet connection 
• If not received within 72 hours of creation, write to: 
 pminternship@mca.gov.in 
 pmisindustrysupport@mca.gov.in 
Include CIN, company name, your name, email, mobile and a brief description. 
15. Do network entities (subsidiaries, vendors, sister companies) need separate 
logins? 
Company Admin can create separate Nodal for each network entity. Each nodal: 
• Receives login credentials. 
Nodal may create HR and Supervisors as per the requirements 
• HR manages the internship postings, selection and joining of interns 
•Supervisors’ marks joining of interns in the portal, uploads training schedule & 
QPR, mark eligibility for monthly assistance etc. Each intern must be mapped with 
some supervisor. 
16. Why can’t I post/edit an internship? 
Common causes & fixes: 
• Role/permission: Only HR can create postings; Camp postings require 
Admin/Nodal approval 
• Company Profile incomplete: About the Company must be filled by Nodal 
Officer 
• Geo tag missing/incorrect: Drop the map pin accurately (zoom in), then fill 
address fields 
• QCS mismatch: Use generic specialisations (e.g., Computer Science, Commerce, 
Mechanical) — avoid overly specific ones (e.g., AI, Mechatronics) 
• Prohibited role: Menial/unskilled roles are blocked 
• Published postings: Once live, postings cannot be edited. Delete and recreate (or 
clone via Fetch Internship ID) if corrections are needed 
• Browser/network: Try Chrome/Edge (latest), switch network, clear cache 
Still stuck? Email screenshots + Internship ID (if any) to: 
 pminternship@mca.gov.in | pmisindustrysupport@mca.gov.in 
17. How do we handle Offer Acceptance and Offer Lapsed scenarios? 
• Candidate must Accept/Decline within 14 days of the Provisional Internship 
Letter 
• Day 15: System marks Offer Lapsed 
• Want to restore? Admin/Nodal Officer can use Status Reversal to move Offer 
Lapsed → Selected by Company, then proceed 
Once the candidate accepts one offer, all other active applications close 
automatically. 
18. How do we confirm joining and trigger the one-time DBT? 
Two confirmations are required: 
1. Supervisor marks Physically Joined (on joining date) 
2. HR clicks Joining Verified by HR 
Plus: Intern’s Aadhaar must be seeded to a bank account (no bank details are 
collected on PMIS). 
Aadhaar seeding check: https://resident.uidai.gov.in/bank-mapper or bank 
branch. 
PMIS Intern can also check seeding status or raise a seeding request on the PMIS 
Portal (Candidate dashboard). 
• Tranche 1 of one-time grant (₹3,000) is released within 15 days after HR 
verification. 
• Tranche 2 (₹3,000) is released within 15 days after 3 months completion + 
company share processed for 2 months. 
19. Where do we upload the company share for Monthly Assistance, and what are 
the rules? 
• HR uploads the Monthly Stipend Excel before the 5th of every month 
Include: 
• amount_from_company (₹900 minimum as per policy; may be pro-rated for 
attendance) 
• addl_amount_from_company (if company pays above minimum) 
System rules: 
• Uploads trigger processing immediately and cannot be edited 
• Incorrect format or missing mandatory fields → system rejection 
• If an intern is auto-eligible (joined at least once in that month) but should not 
receive DBT (e.g., long absence): 
• Do not upload their stipend row, and 
• Admin/Nodal may use Eligibility Reversal in Status Reversal 
20. How do we create Terms & Conditions (T&C) and assign them to Internship

Before taking any selection action:

• Training Schedule: Supervisor → Training Schedule → Create (within first

• Add Training Type, Quarter, Topics, Attachments 
• Assign candidates to schedule

Flexibility, + 2 technical skills

22. How do we mark Dropout or Termination? 
• Dropout (Intern-initiated): Intern requests via portal → Supervisor review → HR

• Termination (Company-initiated): Supervisor marks in My Interns → HR 
verifies & confirms 
• Terminated interns cannot participate in PMIS again 
Do not mark a candidate as dropout if they accepted a PO and joined your 
organization. 
23. What is the Did Not Report (DNR) logic? 
• If a candidate doesn’t join on the reporting date: 
• The system gives a 14-day grace period 
• If still not joined → status changes to DNR automatically 
• After DNR: Candidate becomes inactive; company may proceed to the next 
candidate 
24. Can we reallocate an intern to a different internship ID? 
Yes (Admin only). Use Reallocation if required due to location/role fit or to 
improve outcomes. Evaluate feasibility and document reasons.

Flow: Admin → Company Details → Fill → Preview → Submit

28. What are the best practices to ensure smooth portal operations? 
• Complete About the Company before first posting 
• Use clear, specific titles (no “Intern”, “PMIS Intern”) 
• Avoid over-specific QCS (use generic specializations)

• Set Application End Date correctly (15–30 days Regular; 0–14 days Camp)

• Confirm Aadhaar seeding on joining day to avoid DBT delays 
• Upload stipend Excel before 5th; double check format & amounts 
• Submit QPRs on time with meaningful feedback 
• Close grievances within TATs; escalate when required 
• Use Status Reversal judiciously (Offer Lapsed, Eligibility reversal)

3. Internship Posting 
30. How can Partner Companies post internship opportunities on the PMIS Portal? 
Partner Companies can post internships only through the Internship Management 
module on the PMIS Portal. 
Posting Workflow 
Posting requires the following user hierarchy: 
1. Company Admin 
• Receives login credentials 
• Creates Nodal Officer account 
• Approves Camp postings (final checker) 
2. Nodal Officer 
• Updates company details 
• Creates HR User accounts 
• May also approve postings (if delegated) 
3. HR User (Maker) 
Only HR Users can create internship postings 
Can: 
✓ Create new postings 
✓ Save drafts 
✓ Fetch details from previous postings 
✗ Cannot edit postings once published 
Posting Types Available 
PMIS supports two types of postings: 
1. Regular Internship Posting 
2. Camp Mobilisation Posting (for on-ground recruitment drives) 
Approved postings are visible to all eligible candidates on the portal. 
31. What information must be included in every internship posting? 
Every PMIS internship posting must include accurate, complete, and geo-verified 
details so candidates can apply confidently. 
A. Internship Role Details 
• Internship Title (clear, specific, not generic like “Intern”) 
• Sector (Manufacturing, FMCG, IT, BFSI, etc.) 
• Area/Field (HR, Finance, QA, Supply Chain, Sales, etc.) 
• Application Start & End Date 
• Regular posting: 15–30 days window 
• Camp posting: 0–14 days window 
• Internship Duration (6 or 9 months) 
• Mode (On Site or Hybrid — Remote not allowed) 
• Key Responsibilities 
• Skills Interns Will Acquire 
• Expected Learning Outcomes 
• Keywords 
• Number of Internship Seats 
• Gender Preference (if any; discouraged unless necessary) 
Language to avoid: Do not use “Employment”, “Job”, “Salary”, “Pay”, or “Naukri”. 
Use only “Monthly assistance” or “Financial support” to describe the ₹9,000 
structure. 
B. Location & Geo-Tagging (Mandatory) 
• Address Line 1 & 2 
• State, District, Block, Village/Town 
• PIN Code 
• Live Geo Tag using map coordinates 
Note: Incorrect geo-tagging reduces visibility and increases dropout rates. 
C. Required Qualifications (QCS Rule Engine) 
You must select: 
• Qualification Level (10th, 12th, ITI, Diploma, Graduate, PG) 
• Course (BA, BCom, BSc, BBA, BTech, etc.) 
• Specialization (must be generic) 
 Use: Computer Science 
 Avoid: Artificial Intelligence / Mechatronics 
• Preferred skills & certifications 
• Additional notes (if any) 
D. Additional Benefits 
Companies may specify: 
• Additional financial assistance (above mandatory ₹900/month) 
• Meals (Free/Subsidized) 
• Accommodation/Hostel 
• Transportation 
• Health & wellness benefits 
• Access to recreational facilities 
• Any other benefits 
E. Optional but Strongly Recommended 
• Internship Description Video 
• Infographics/Images 
• Intern Journey Videos 
F. Camp Mobilisation Only (Extra Required Fields) 
• Campus Name 
• Date & Time 
• Coordinator Name, Designation, Mobile, Email 
• Full Camp Address (with geo-tag) 
32. Can Partner Companies edit or remove internship postings? 
Regular Internship Posting 
 Once published, postings cannot be edited. 
HR Users may only: 
• Edit while in Draft 
• Delete before publishing 
• Clone a posting via “Fetch Internship ID” to create a corrected version 
Camp Posting 
✔ HR can submit 
✔ Admin/Nodal Officer must approve

• Delete the posting 
• Create a new one with corrected information 
33. What type of internship roles are allowed or prohibited under PMIS? 
Allowed Roles — roles that: 
• Build skills 
• Provide meaningful work exposure 
• Offer structured training 
• Include mentorship 
• Enhance employability 
Examples: 
• Marketing Intern 
• Data Analyst Intern 
• Supply Chain Intern 
• Finance Intern 
• QA Intern 
• Design Intern 
• Field Research Intern 
• Manufacturing Support Intern 
STRICTLY PROHIBITED (Unskilled / Menial Roles) 
PMIS does NOT permit roles such as: 
• Cleaner, Sweeper, Janitor 
• Delivery Agent, Driver 
• Helper, Labourer, Loader 
• Office Boy, Peon 
• Security Guard 
• Gardener, Landscaper 
• Courier 
• Car Washer 
• Picker/Sorter 
• Steward trainee 
• Bellboy, Porter, Usher 
Any role that does not impart meaningful skills 
Requirement for All Allowed Roles 
Every posting must clearly define: 
• Purpose of the role 
• Significance within business operations 
• Technical & behavioural skills gained 
• Real world exposure 
• Team interactions 
• Expected responsibilities

5. Internship Details 
38. Can the internship be extended beyond the prescribed duration of 6 or 9 
months? 
No. Under the PMIS Pilot Project Extension: 
• New internships are only of 6 months or 9 months duration. 
• Existing Round 1/2 interns will continue with 12-month duration. 
• Extensions are not allowed, except in the case of approved sabbaticals. 
Exception — Sabbatical Extension 
If an intern takes a sabbatical (up to 2 months maximum), their internship duration 
is extended only to cover the sabbatical period. 
Example: 
• A 6-month internship + 1-month sabbatical → Total duration becomes 7 months. 
No extensions beyond the sabbatical-related adjustment are permitted. 
39. Is it mandatory for companies to provide training to interns? 
Yes. Absolutely mandatory. 
As per guidelines: 
• Companies must ensure relevant, structured training for each intern. 
Training must align with: 
• The internship role 
• Skills the intern is expected to learn 
• Sector/functional requirements 
Mandatory Requirements for Training 
• A Training Schedule must be created by the Supervisor within the first month. 
• Companies must ensure at least 50% of internship time is in real-world work, not 
classroom sessions. 
• Training costs must be borne by the company (CSR/own funds). 
40. Are companies required to assign supervisors or mentors to interns? 
Yes. Mandatory. 
Each intern must be assigned a Supervisor upon joining. 
Supervisor Responsibilities 
• Confirm physical joining on portal 
• Create and assign Training Schedule 
• Guide daily tasks & workplace learning 
• Provide mentorship, feedback, and support 
• Submit quarterly QPR (Quarterly Progress Report) 
• Initiate termination/dropout requests if required 
Good Practice: Companies are encouraged to strengthen employability skills, 
professional behaviour, and soft skills as part of the intern’s journey. 
Note: For 6 or 9 month internships, Supervisors manage the intern for the entire 
duration, not 12 months. 
41. Do interns qualify as employees under labour codes? 
No. They do not. 
As clarified by Ministry of Labour & Employment (OM dated 27.09.2024) and PMIS 
guidelines: 
• Interns under PMIS cannot be treated as employees. 
No employer-employee relationship is created with: 
• The Ministry 
• The company 
• Any group/network entity 
Interns therefore cannot claim: 
• Employment benefits 
• Social security benefits 
• Continuity of service 
• Automatic job rights 
Good Practice: PMIS internships are learning-oriented, not employment contracts. 
42. What are the sources of onboarding interns for this scheme? 
Interns must be sourced exclusively through the PMIS Portal. 
Portal-Driven Selection Flow: 
1. Candidates register → Aadhaar authentication 
2. Portal generates a system resume 
3. Candidates apply for opportunities 
4. PMIS shortlists applications automatically based on QCS + preferences 
5. Shortlist sent to the company 
6. Company evaluates, interviews, and selects 
7. System issues Provisional Internship Letter 
8. Candidate accepts/declines through portal 
9. Joining is recorded through Supervisor + HR verification 
 Offline or internal selection is not permitted. 
 Companies cannot onboard interns outside the PMIS portal. 
43. Does an internship offer create an employer–employee relationship? 
No. 
An internship offer: 
• Does not create any legal or employment relationship 
• Does not entitle interns to employment benefits 
• Is not equivalent to a job offer 
• Cannot be used to claim any rights under labour laws 
This is clearly stated in Guidelines 5.5.6. 
44. Can an internship offer be construed as a promise of future employment? 
No. 
The internship offer: 
• Is not a guarantee of employment 
• Is not a commitment for future job placement 
• Cannot be interpreted as pre-employment training 
 However, companies may voluntarily offer: 
• PPOs (after 50% duration), or 
• POs (after 50% duration, with immediate joining) 
…but these are optional and based on company discretion. 
45. Are interns required to follow the rules and regulations of the company? 
Yes. 
Interns must adhere to all company policies related to: 
• Working hours 
• Leave, attendance, and punctuality 
• Conduct, ethics, and discipline 
• Workplace safety 
• Confidentiality & data handling rules 
• Dress code, behaviour, and other internal norms 
Non-compliance may lead to: 
• Warning 
• Suspension (company-level) 
• Termination (documented on PMIS portal) 
 Terminated interns cannot participate in PMIS again. 
46. Are companies required to provide accommodation, transport, canteen, or 
similar facilities? 
No. It is not mandatory. 
Companies may provide such facilities if they choose to. 
Optional benefits include: 
• Accommodation/Hostel 
• Transportation 
• Food/Canteen 
• Health & Wellness 
• Insurance (additional to Govt-provided PMJJBY/PMSBY equivalents) 
• Recreational facilities 
• Additional financial assistance 
If such benefits exist, they must be clearly specified in the Internship Posting under 
“Additional Benefits.” 
 Companies offering strong benefits receive preference during new-company 
onboarding (as per Round 3 SOP). 
47. Can interns take leave during internship? 
Yes, but must follow company’s internal leave rules. 
Additionally — Sabbatical Leave 
Allowed only for: 
• Medical emergencies 
• Death of a family member 
• Extenuating conditions: 
• Maximum 2 months 
• Internship duration is extended to cover sabbatical 
• No financial assistance during sabbatical 
• Requires Supervisor → HR → Portal approval 
48. What happens if an intern drops out or is terminated? 
Dropout (Intern-initiated) 
• Registered through portal 
• Requires company approval 
• Intern gets only one more chance to rejoin PMIS 
• After second dropout → permanently ineligible 
Termination (Company-initiated) 
• Due to misconduct, prolonged absenteeism, or performance issues 
• Supervisor marks → HR verifies → Portal records 
• Terminated intern → cannot participate in PMIS again 
49. Are companies required to upload performance evaluations? 
Yes. Mandatory. 
Companies must upload:

• HR reviews & approves 
These documents are essential for: 
• DBT continuation

• Certificate eligibility 
• Future PPO/PO decision-making 
50. Can companies rotate interns between departments? 
Yes, if:

• The core internship role remains aligned to the posting 
Note: If rotation fundamentally changes job role, Admin can reallocate to a different 
Internship ID using Reallocation.

Yes. 
Participation Certificate

Completion Certificate 
• After 100% duration 
• After company marks “Internship Completed”

• Download enabled only after intern submits final survey

Companies must maintain:

6. Rolling Process – Overview 
53. What does the Rolling Selection Model mean for Partner Companies? 
Under the Rolling Model, each internship posting progresses independently, with 
its own: 
• application window 
• shortlisting event 
• 45 day selection clock 
• offer acceptance window 
• joining timeline 
There is no common selection round. 
Each Internship Opportunity (IO) runs its own timeline from Day P → Day E → Day 
E+45 → Day J. 
7. Internship Posting & Application Window 
54. What happens on Day P (Posting Creation)? 
Day P = The date HR publishes the internship posting. 
On Day P: 
• Internship becomes immediately visible to candidates 
• HR cannot edit once posting goes live 
• Application End Date must be set between Day P + 15 to 30 days 
• Only drafts can be edited, not published postings 
55. How long do candidates have to apply? (Day P → Day E) 
The application window stays open for 15–30 days (Regular posting) or 0–14 days 
(Camp Posting). 
During this period: 
• Candidates may apply (max 3 active applications at a time) 
• Companies may see number of exact-match candidates, but no shortlist yet 
• Applications continue to accumulate until Day E 
8. Shortlisting (Day E) 
56. What happens on Day E (Application End Date)? 
On Day E, the system automatically: 
1. Closes applications 
2. Runs the shortlisting engine (only once) 
3. Generates a shortlist based on QCS + diversity rules 
4. Sends the shortlist to the company 
Shortlist Size Rule: 
Max shortlist size = 2 × number of exact matches 
If fewer exact matches exist, only those are shared. 
Companies cannot request new shortlists. 
Note: Non-exact match applications are visible to candidates for transparency, 
but only exact matches are eligible for shortlisting under the QCS rule engine. 
9. Selection (Day E+1 → Day E+45) 
57. How long do companies have to act on the shortlist? 
Partner Companies have 45 days from Day E to take actions: 
• Select candidate 
• Waitlist candidate 
• Reject with reason 
• Mark “Did Not Appear” 
If the company does NOT act on an application by Day E+45: 
System automatically marks it as: 
➡ Not considered by company 
➡ Candidate’s application slot is freed 
This prevents blocking candidates. 
58. Can a company change the shortlist or request new candidates? 
No. The Rolling Model generates only one shortlist per Internship ID. 
If a company needs more candidates: 
• HR must create a new posting 
• The new posting will run its own 15–30 day cycle 
10. Offer Stage (Day O → Day O+14) 
59. How long does a candidate have to accept an offer? 
Candidates have 14 days from the date of offer (Day O). 
Status outcomes: 
• Accept: Moves to Offer Accepted; all other applications auto-close 
• Decline: Offer becomes inactive 
• No action by Day O+15: Offer Lapsed 
Admin/Nodal may use Status Reversal → Offer Lapsed → Selected by Company 
(if the company wants to proceed). 
60. How many offers can a candidate hold? 
A candidate may: 
• receive up to 2 offers 
• accept only 1 
• after acceptance, all other applications auto-close 
11. Joining & Onboarding (Day J → +1 Month → +14 Days) 
61. What is the Joining Window in the Rolling Process? 
Day J = Reporting date in the Provisional Internship Letter. 
Before Day J: 
Supervisor may grant up to 1 month extension (only once). 
After Day J + 14 days: 
If intern still has not joined → System marks Did Not Report (DNR). 
62. What happens if companies delay marking joining? 
Delayed Supervisor/HR actions → delayed DBT. 
Financial assistance begins only when: 
1. Supervisor marks Physically Joined, and 
2. HR marks Joining Verified by HR 
If not marked on time: 
• DBT may not trigger 
• Intern experience gets affected 
• Companies may receive grievances 
63. Can companies withdraw an offer after it is accepted? 
Only under specific reasons: 
• Candidate Not Eligible 
• DNR 
• Termination (post joining) 
The company must record a valid reason. 
12. Internship Lifecycle (Rolling) 
64. What happens after the intern joins? 
The Rolling Model does not change the lifecycle: 
• One-time DBT (Tranche 1 & 2) 
• Monthly DBT (company upload → govt DBT) 
• Training Schedule (within 30 days) 
• QPR every quarter 
• Dropout / Termination functionality 
• Sabbatical (max 2 months) 
13. Completion, Certification & Placement 
65. When does the Participation Certificate unlock? 
After 50% of internship duration. 
66. When do PPO/PO rules unlock? 
Also after 50% completion: 
• 6 month internship → after 3 months 
• 9 month internship → after 4.5 months 
67. When does the Completion Certificate unlock? 
After: 
• 100% duration, AND 
• Company marks Internship Completed in the portal 
14. Candidate Journey (Rolling Model) 
68. How does the Rolling Model change the candidate’s journey? 
Candidates can now: 
• Apply instantly when a posting is published 
• Hold max 3 active applications 
• Receive shortlists independently for each posting 
• Hold max 2 offers 
• Accept 1 offer 
• Join on flexible dates (subject to supervisor approval) 
• Enter internships continuously rather than in batches 
This drastically improves candidate mobility and reduces wait times. 
15. Joining Process 
69. Can a candidate be marked as “Candidate Not Eligible” during the onboarding 
process? 
Yes. Supervisors can mark a candidate as “Candidate Not Eligible” under specific 
legitimate scenarios: 
• Invalid / forged documents 
• Incomplete onboarding documents 
• Candidate found working full-time elsewhere 
• Candidate not meeting eligibility criteria (e.g., family income, qualifications) 
• Candidate fails Aadhaar-based verification 
• Severe misrepresentation of information 
 This is a protective measure to ensure compliance with PMIS guidelines. 
70. What happens after marking a candidate as “Candidate Not Eligible”? 
• The case is automatically forwarded to MCA for manual review 
• MCA verifies documentation & reasons 
Based on assessment, MCA may: 
• Revoke the offer (most likely), or 
• Allow the candidate to proceed in rare justified cases 
 Until MCA decides, the candidate’s onboarding is paused. 
71. What additional information must HR add to the Provisional Internship 
Letter? 
To ensure clarity for candidates, the following details are mandatory: 
• Reporting Address (complete, with PIN) 
• Reporting Date 
• Reporting Time (if applicable) 
• Contact Person Name 
• Contact Person Mobile Number 
• Alternate Contact Number (landline or mobile) 
• Any specific instructions related to joining formalities 
These reduce confusion, improve reporting rates, and reduce DNR cases. 
72. What confirmations must be completed on the joining date for DBT to begin? 
Two mandatory confirmations: 
1. Supervisor → Physically Joined 
2. HR → Joining Verified by HR 
Both must be completed for: 
• First ₹3,000 (One-time Grant – Tranche 1) 
• Monthly DBT eligibility 
PLUS: Intern’s Aadhaar must be seeded with their bank account. 
No joining = No DBT. 
73. What if Aadhaar is not linked to a bank when the intern joins? 
DBT will fail. 
Intern must: 
• Seed Aadhaar with a bank account 
• Check status at: https://resident.uidai.gov.in/bank-mapper 
• Inform HR once updated 
HR/Supervisor must validate seeding on joining day to prevent delays. 
74. Can interns request a change in internship location or role after accepting the 
offer? 
Yes — Admin can approve Reallocation if: 
• Change is necessary due to genuine medical/personal reasons 
• Intern is better suited to another role 
• Facility constraints arise 
Reallocation is not automatic and must be justified. 
75. What happens if an intern must join late due to relocation or personal 
constraints? 
Supervisor may approve Joining Later with a new date: 
• Within 1 month limit 
• Only before the original joining date 
• Must be recorded on portal 
• All downstream DBT timelines adjust accordingly 
16. Internship Journey 
76. How does the company confirm an intern’s participation in the scheme? 
Intern participation is confirmed through a two-step joining verification: 
Step 1 — Supervisor Action 
• Supervisor marks the intern as Physically Joined. 
Step 2 — HR Action 
• HR verifies the joining using Joining Verified by HR. 
Both confirmations are mandatory for: 
• Release of One Time DBT (Tranche 1) 
• Initiation of Monthly Assistance (DBT) 
• Allowing intern to appear in Training, QPR, and Allowance dashboards 
No joining = No DBT. 
77. Can the joining date be modified after the intern has already joined? 
Yes, but only by MCA. 
If a joining date needs correction due to: 
• Technical error 
• Incorrect entry by Supervisor/HR 
• Discrepancy between physical joining and portal date 
Then companies must submit: 
• A formal written request to MCA 
• Valid justification 
• Supporting documents (e.g., attendance logs, joining forms) 
If approved, MCA will correct the joining date. 
78. What mechanisms exist to deal with misbehaviour, underperformance, or 
absenteeism? 
Companies can take the following actions through the portal: 
A. Mark for Termination (Supervisor → HR) 
Used for: 
• Persistent poor performance 
• Violation of company rules 
• Misconduct, harassment, or behaviour issues 
B. Mark as Absconding 
Used when intern: 
• Stops attending work without notice 
• Ignores two documented contact attempts 
• Remains unreachable/unresponsive 
C. Mark as Ineligible (Onboarding stage only) 
Workflow: Supervisor → Initiates action; HR → Reviews & Confirms 
Once confirmed: 
• DBT stops 
• Intern becomes inactive 
• Terminated interns cannot rejoin PMIS again 
• Absconding interns cannot receive certificates 
79. Do companies need to create a monitoring & evaluation framework for 
interns? 
Yes. Mandatory. 
PMIS requires companies to monitor interns through: 
A. Training Schedule 
• Supervisor creates it within first 30 days 
• Must align with actual job role 
• Must ensure 50% of internship is real-work exposure 
B. Quarterly Progress Report (QPR) 
Completed every quarter: 
Supervisor Inputs: 
• Punctuality 
• Teamwork 
• Communication 
• Adaptability 
• Two technical skills 
• Performance comments 
HR Actions: 
• Reviews QPR 
• Adds remarks 
• Approves final submission 
The QPR is critical for: 
• Quality assurance 
• Internship continuation 
• PPO/PO considerations 
• Compliance with PMIS guidelines 
80. What happens if an intern withdraws or drops out before completion? 
If an intern wishes to withdraw: 
1. Intern submits Dropout Request via portal 
2. Supervisor reviews and approves/rejects 
3. HR validates and confirms 
After confirmation: 
• Remaining DBT stops 
• Intern becomes inactive 
• Intern gets one more chance to join PMIS in future 
• Participation Certificate may be issued only if ≥ 50% duration completed 
81. What are the criteria for marking an intern as Absconding? 
An intern may be marked as Absconding when they: 
• Suddenly stop attending internship without informing 
• Do not respond despite two follow-up attempts (recommended gap: 7 days each) 
• Do not resume work even after reminders 
Consequences: 
• Intern becomes inactive 
• No certificates will be issued 
• Company need not pay any further allowance 
• Government DBT stops automatically 
82. Will the company’s leave policy govern interns? 
Yes. Interns must follow all internal leave rules related to:

• Casual leave 
• Attendance 
• Working hours 
• Holidays 
• Behaviour and discipline 
Companies may frame an intern-specific leave policy if required. 
83. Can interns take a sabbatical? 
Yes, in exceptional/emergency cases, such as: 
• Major medical issues 
• Death of a family member 
• Critical, documented emergencies 
Conditions: 
• Maximum 2 months 
• No financial assistance (DBT or company share) during sabbatical 
• Internship duration gets extended by the sabbatical period 
 Example: 6-month internship + 1-month sabbatical → 7 months total 
Approval Workflow: Intern → Supervisor → HR → Portal Approval 
Note: 
• Supervisor/HR must upload supporting documents 
• Status is updated to Sabbatical on the portal 
84. What if a sabbatical exceeds two months? 
If sabbatical exceeds 2 months:

18. Financial Assistance To Interns 
96. What makes interns eligible for receiving the DBT and monthly internship 
allowance? 
An intern becomes eligible ONLY when all three conditions are fulfilled: 
1. Physically Joined (marked by Supervisor) 
2. Joining Verified by HR 
3. Aadhaar seeded bank account 
For Monthly Allowance: 
• Supervisor must mark intern as Eligible in Internship Allowance section 
• HR must: 
• Download the month’s Excel file 
• Enter company payment details (amount_from_company + additional amount) 
• Upload the Excel in the same format 
• Ensure company payment is completed 
Once the company payment is validated, the government’s share is automatically 
released via DBT. 
97. What is the breakup of the monthly financial assistance provided to interns? 
As per revised guidelines (effective for the Pilot Extension): 
Total Monthly Assistance: ₹9,000 
• Company Share: ₹900 per month 
• Paid from CSR or company funds 
• Can be proportional based on attendance/HR policies 
• Government Share (DBT): ₹8,100 per month 
• Paid directly to Aadhaar seeded bank account 
• Pro-rated based on company payment 
Important: 
If the intern is from older 12-month batches, from March 2026 onward, they will 
also follow the updated ₹9,000/month model. 
98. Can a company give more than the mandatory ₹900 per month? 
Yes. Companies may offer additional financial assistance from their own funds. 
HR must capture this under: 
• addl_amount_from_company in the stipend Excel 
The system records it for transparency and reporting. 
99. Does issuing a PPO/PO impact DBT eligibility? 
No. As long as the intern: 
• continues internship till completion 
• maintains attendance 
• continues to be “Eligible” in internship allowance section 
DBT continues normally. Joining the PPO/PO before internship completion is 
treated as Placement and NOT dropout. 
This does not change certificate rules — a Completion Certificate still requires 
100% internship completion. 
100. Is there a deadline for companies to make their monthly payments? 
Yes. Company share must be: 
• Paid every month, and 
• Uploaded on PMIS portal BEFORE the 5th of every month 
Government DBT is triggered only after company payment is uploaded. 
101. How should payments be calculated if an intern is absent for several days? 
Internship allowance should be pro-rated as per: 
• Company attendance policy 
• Number of working days completed 
• Hours worked (if the company uses a time-based system) 
HR must enter the pro-rated amount in the Excel sheet. 
102. Is the intern eligible for the previous month’s allowance if they were marked 
absconding/terminated in the current month? 
Yes. If an intern was: 
• Joining Verified by HR during that earlier month 
• And eligible for payment that month 
…they will receive the allowance for that previous month (pro-rated if needed), 
even if they exit in the following month. 
103. What is the complete monthly internship allowance process?

Step 2 — HR: 
• Downloads the official Excel template 
• Enters: 
• Company share 
• Additional assistance (optional) 
• Uploads the sheet (same format required) 
• Confirms company payment is completed 
Step 3 — System: 
• Government share (DBT) is disbursed automatically 
This entire process is detailed in the Internship Allowance Guidelines. 
104. Some interns have not received DBT. What should the company do?

• Incorrect Excel entries

• Supervisor did not mark eligibility

• Aadhaar seeded account 
If any condition is missing, the Supervisor cannot mark them eligible. 
If all conditions are correct and still inaccessible → email PMIS support. 
106. How can an incorrect eligibility marking be corrected? 
Use Status Reversal feature: 
• Company Admin can reverse: 
• Eligible → Ineligible 
• Ineligible → Eligible 
Must provide: 
• Valid reason 
• Supporting document 
107. Should interns who left the program still receive allowance? 
No — for the current month. 
If marked: 
• Terminated 
• Absconding 
• Dropped 
Supervisor must mark them Ineligible in the Allowance section. 
Yes — for the previous month 
…if their joining was verified in that earlier month. 
19. Insurance Coverage To Interns 
108. Are companies required to provide insurance to interns? 
No. Not mandatory. 
The Government of India provides all interns with: 
• PMJJBY-equivalent Life Insurance 
• PMSBY-equivalent Accidental Insurance 
Premium is paid fully by the Government. 
Companies MAY provide additional insurance: 
• Accidental coverage 
• Medical insurance 
• Group insurance benefits 
…but this is optional and based on the company’s internal policy. 
20. PPO/PO & Employment Transition 
109. Can interns receive PPO/PO from other companies (non-PMIS)? 
Yes. If an intern accepts a full-time offer elsewhere, companies must: 
• record “Joined Full-Time Role – Other Company” in PPO/PO module 
• mention the organization name 
• ensure correct internship exit logic 
• allow intern to receive Participation Certificate (if ≥50% completed)

 State FAQs 
1. General Information 
1. What is the Prime Minister’s Internship Scheme (PMIS)? 
PMIS is a Government of India initiative, anchored by the Ministry of Corporate 
Affairs (MCA), to provide 6 or 9 month internships in top companies through a 
centralized digital portal and mobile app. The scheme aims to strengthen 
employability by exposing youth to real ‑world business environments. Existing 
interns from earlier pilot rounds may continue at 12 months as notified. 
2. Does PMIS guarantee jobs? 
No. 
PMIS is designed to enhance employability via structured internships; employment is 
not guaranteed. Partner companies may extend job offers at their discretion; an 
internship offer does not create an employer–employee relationship with MCA or the 
company. 
2. Mandate & Governance 
3. Who governs PMIS and how is it structured? 
Ministry of Corporate Affairs . A Monitoring & Steering Committee (MSC) oversees 
design, implementation, and guideline modifications. Implementation is end ‑to‑end 
via the PMIS portal with integrations for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Regional 
Directors (RDs) and Registrars of Companies (RoC) conduct targeted mobilization 
with States Nodal Departments and Partner Companies. 
3. Roles & Responsibilities (State & District) 
4. What is the State focal points and their responsibilities? 
• State Nodal Department (SND): Ensures convergence with Skill, Higher 
Education, Industries, Labor Departments and any other department that the State 
Nodal Officer finds applicable for better mobilization and implementation. 
• State Nodal Officer (SNO) & State PMIS Cell: Coordinate outreach/mobilization, 
run weekly MIS reviews, manage escalations, and liaison with MCA/RD/RoC. 
• District Nodal Officer (DNO): Drive institute outreach (ITIs, polytechnics, 
colleges), employment exchanges, and on‑ground counselling. 
4. Onboarding & Access 
5. What do candidates need to register? 
• Free registration through Aadhaar e‑KYC on the PMIS portal/app. 
• Provide personal, educational, and contact details; upload required documents in 
PDF format. 
• Portal supports multiple Indian languages; OTP/login help, manuals, videos, and 
chatbot are available to support the candidate in the registration process. 
6. How should login/OTP or performance issues be handled? 
• Refresh page; check connectivity; try incognito or another browser; clear cache; 
disable extensions; try another device. 
• If unresolved, file a Complaint on the portal (Portal/DBT/Internship category) or 
call the Helpline 1800‑11‑6090. 
5. Eligibility & Ineligibility 
7. Who should States mobilize? 
• Indian citizens aged 18–25 (on application date), not in full‑time employment. 
• Education: 10th/12th, ITI, Diploma, UG/PG 
(e.g.BA/B.Sc./B.Com/BCA/BBA/B.E./B.Tech; 
MA/M.Sc./M.Com/MCA/M.Tech). 
• Online/distance learners and final ‑year full‑time students are eligible as notified 
in the PMIS guidelines. 
8. Who are ineligible? 
• Graduates/postgraduates from IITs, IIMs, IIITs, IISc, IISER, NLUs, NITs. 
• Holders of CA, CMA, CS, MBBS, BDS, MD, MS, MBA/equivalent, MPhil, PhD. 
• Those in any Central/State skill/apprenticeship/internship/training scheme 
including NATS/NAPS; or who have completed NATS/NAPS. 
• Family income (self/parents/spouse) more than ₹12 lakh in preceding F inancial 
Year; or any permanent/regular Government employee in the family. 
6. Rolling Application & Offer Process 
9. What are the key system limits and shortlist rules? 
• Maximum active applications per candidate: 3; maximum concurrent offers: 2; 
only 1 offer can be accepted. 
• Offer action window: 14 days to Accept/Reject; no action → Offer Lapsed on day 
15. 
• Before shortlist, companies see only the count of exact ‑match applications; at 
window close the portal shares a single shortlist up to 2× exact matches. 
• Companies should complete selection within 30 days from application window 
end. 
7. Internship Lifecycle 
10. What are joining and DNR rules? 
• Candidate must join on/before the reporting date in the offer letter. 
• Supervisor may grant a joining extension up to 1 month (requested before original 
report date). 
• If not joined by reporting/extended date, a 14 ‑day grace applies; system 
auto‑marks DNR on day 15. 
11. Are status reversals allowed? 
Limited reversal windows (typically 14 –15 days) exist for Offer Lapsed, DNR, 
Rejected, and No‑Show. 
12. What is the sabbatical policy? 
Sabbatical may be granted for genuine emergencies; combined breaks up to 2 months; 
no financial assistance during sabbatical; internship end date extends accordingly; 
re‑joining permitted as per approvals on portal. 
13. What about Dropout and Termination? 
• Dropout: Intern gets one additional chance to participate again. A second dropout 
disqualifies further participation. 
• Termination by company (performance/misconduct as per policy): Intern is not 
permitted to participate again. 
8. Financial Assistance & Insurance 
14. What assistance is paid to interns? 
• Monthly assistance: ₹9,000 (Company ₹900 + Government up to ₹8,100 via DBT to 
Aadhaar‑seeded account). Government share is proportionate to company 
payment. 
• One‑time incidental grant: ₹6,000 in two tranches of ₹3,000 each. The first tranche 
will be disbursed within 15 days of confirmation of joining the internship. The 
second tranche (i.e. Rs. 3000/ -) will be released within 15 days of completion of 3 
months of internship. 
Q15. What about insurance? 
Government provides insurance equivalent to PMJJBY and PMSBY; companies may 
optionally extend additional accidental cover. Insurance is applied once per intern via 
the portal’s single‑payment logic. 
9. Grievances & Helpline 
16. How can intern raise grievances? 
• Use the in‑portal “Complaints” section: choose category (DBT/Internship/Portal), 
add details and evidence, and track status. 
• Self‑help resources: User Manuals, Guidance Videos, and Chatbot on the portal. 
• Helpline: 1800‑11‑6090 (multilingual). 
10. Monitoring 
17. What should States review weekly? 
• Registrations and e ‑KYC readiness; profiles initiated vs. completed; 
Aadhaar‑seeding readiness. 
• Active applications; offers pending action within 14 days; Offer Lapsed. 
• Joining on time vs. extensions; DNR cases; Dropouts/Terminations; Sabbaticals. 
• Complaints volume and resolution TAT (DBT/Internship/Portal). 
18. Filters, rounds, and downloads 
• Filters: District (across views); plus company, sector, internship ID, status where 
available. 
• Round selector (where shown) switches cohort/period for tables. 
• Each table/section provides a Download button for CSV/Excel/PDF as 
configured. For State‑wide extracts, raise a Support Request via the portal. 
11. Communications & Outreach 
Use only official scheme facts (eligibility, durations, financials). Avoid job ‑guarantee 
language. Promote helpline and complaints flow. 
12. Contacts & Support 
• State PMIS Cell / SNO – State‑level coordination, district support. 
• Portal Complaints (recommended first line) and Helpline 1800 ‑11‑6090 for tickets 
and assistance. 
• RD/RoC (MCA field) – joint mobilization drives with State; local industry 
connects. 
13. User Management – Creating State & District User IDs 
A) Create the State Admin user ID 
1. What details are required to create the State Admin ID? 
Provide the following (from the State’s official email / letterhead as applicable): 
• Name 
• Designation 
• Mobile number 
• Email ID 
• State/UT Name 
2. Where do I send these details? 
Share from the official State ID to pminternship@mca.gov.in requesting creation of 
the State Admin user. 
B) Create additional State user IDs (by the State Admin) 
3. How many State user IDs can the State Admin create? 
The State Admin can create up to 5 additional State user IDs. If you need more, 
raise a request to pminternship@mca.gov.in 
4. Steps to add a State user ID (by the State Admin) 
1. Log in as State Admin 
2. Go to Admin/Users → State Users → Add State User 
3. Enter: Name, Designation, Mobile Number, Email ID 
4. Save → user receives login setup/OTP on the registered email/mobile (portal 
password rules apply). 
o Passwords must meet portal complexity (min length and mix of 
upper/lowercase, number, special char). If the user cannot set a 
password or OTP isn’t received, use the portal help or the helpline 
1800-11-6090. 
5. What if we need more than 5 State user IDs? 
Email pminternship@mca.gov.in with justification. 
C) Create District user IDs (by the State Nodal user) 
6. Who can create District users and how many? 
The State Nodal user can create up to 3 user IDs per district through the portal. 
7. Steps to add a District user ID (by the State Nodal user) 
1. Log in as State Nodal 
2. Go to District Users → Add District User 
3. Fill the required fields: 
o District (select from list) 
o User ID (unique username) 
o Name 
o Designation 
o Mobile Number 
o Email ID 
4. Save → the user receives OTP/setup to activate the account (use portal 
troubleshooting/helpline if needed). 
D) Governance, security & hygiene (quick checklist) 
• One person, one account. Do not share IDs or passwords. 
• Use official emails for State/District roles to ensure continuity when staff 
changes. 
• Deprovision on transfer/exit: Remove or reassign IDs immediately; if the user 
has left and you can’t recover access, email PMIS support to revoke. 
• Password/OTP issues: Follow portal troubleshooting steps (refresh, cache 
clear, incognito, alternate browser/network, resend OTP)
