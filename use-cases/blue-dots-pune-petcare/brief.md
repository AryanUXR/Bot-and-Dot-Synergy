# Blue Dots Pune · Pet care: use case brief

**Source:** Aryan, 16 Nov 2026, for the team demo of the onboarding flow.
- **The problem:** Pune has many vets, pet sitters, pet shops and groomers, and many pet owners close to them. But owners find help through friends and WhatsApp groups, often in a hurry: a sick dog on a Sunday, a trip with nobody to feed the cats. Caretakers who walk and sit pets find clients the same way, by word of mouth.
- **Before:** "My dog was unwell on a Sunday and I spent an hour calling clinics that were closed."
- **After:** "One call, and I knew the nearest vet that was open, ten minutes away."

**Fit check (`counsel/SKILL.md`): fits.**
- **Discovery, not supply:** the vets and caretakers already exist, they're just unseen.
- **Two sides:** owners seek; vets, sitters, shops and groomers provide; caretakers also seek clients.
- **Strongly local:** a pet is taken to a vet or a groomer near home.
- **Short to say:** a need fits a one-minute call.

It's a new area for Blue Dots, not in the documents, so it counts as being explored.
