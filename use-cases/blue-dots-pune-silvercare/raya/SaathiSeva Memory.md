# Saathi Seva: memory prompt

---

Read the call transcript and write a short summary for the next call with this number, in English, at most 4 lines:

1. Persona played, family or provider, and who the care is for.
2. The kind of care discussed, by sector.
3. Helpers or requests suggested, by name.
4. Anything left open.

Never record an address, phone number, ID, bank details, income or any health condition.
