# Golden Dots Patna: use case brief

**Sources:**
- The Golden Dots AI infographic.
- The "Golden Data Initiative" note.

**The problem:**
- Citizens and businesses can't find who will help them with documents, schemes and local support.
- SHGs can't reach the people they're equipped to help, and they struggle to get the services they need themselves.
- Before: "I kept hearing about these schemes, but I didn't know who could help me."
- After: "I found a Jeevika Didi just a few minutes away."

**Steer (12 Nov 2026):** don't limit it to Jeevika didis. The network covers every kind of facilitator: SHGs and their federations, document and service counters, welfare desks, skilling providers, health and disability support, banking points, business support, and NGO or CSR partners.

```
## 1. Programme       Golden Dots Patna
## 2. Colour          Gold: primary #A8790F (fills, buttons), dark #5E4306 (headings, text on white); white text only on the dark shade or large text
## 3. Geography       Patna district, Bihar: city wards plus outer blocks (Danapur, Phulwari Sharif, Fatuha, Masaurhi, Bihta, Maner...)
## 4. Seekers         Three kinds:
                      - citizens looking for schemes, documents or services they may be eligible for;
                      - small businesses looking for registration, credit or market links;
                      - SHGs surfacing their own needs (training, buyers, credit).
## 5. Providers       SHGs and village organisations (including Jeevika didis), document and certificate counters,
                      scheme help desks, skilling and job centres, health and disability support, banking points,
                      business support, NGOs and CSR partners
## 6. Bot name        Sahayika (सहायिका), female voice
## 7. Language        Hindi
## 8. Personas        see personas.md: a citizen, a small business, and an SHG leader who both helps others and needs help
## 9. Sectors         Documents & Certificates · Schemes & Welfare Help · SHGs & Jeevika Didis · Skilling & Jobs ·
                      Health & Disability · Banking & Credit · Business Support · NGOs & Community Support · Requests
## 10. Map "You"      the citizen persona
## 11. Data           fictional sample data (rule 1); real public scheme names and national helplines only
## 12. Stack          the tested EkStep stack: Raya, Lovable (remix of Sakhi and Red Dots), Google Maps, Vertex images
```
