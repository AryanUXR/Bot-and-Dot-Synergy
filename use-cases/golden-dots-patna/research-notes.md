## 2. Service types: what they typically offer and where

Each "Where" column is a plausibility judgement, not a list of real places.

### 2.1 Documents & Certificates
- RTPS counters at block, anchal and subdivision offices: caste, income and residence certificates, applied for and tracked under the Bihar Right to Public Services Act (online through [serviceonline.bihar.gov.in](https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in))
- Aadhaar Seva Kendra and bank or post-office Aadhaar centres: new enrolment, mobile/address update, biometric update for children
- Common Service Centres (CSC / Vasudha Kendra): PAN, e-Shram, Ayushman card, scheme forms, bill payments
- Ration card help at the block supply office (MO): applying for a new card, adding a family member, e-KYC
- Typical "help desk" support: filling forms, scanning and uploading documents, explaining which document is needed

**Where:** every block HQ (Phulwari, Danapur, Fatuha, Maner, Bihta, Masaurhi, Naubatpur, Punpun, Sampatchak, Paliganj). Urban CSCs and Aadhaar centres are in Kankarbagh, Rajendra Nagar, Patna City and Boring Road.

### 2.2 Schemes & Welfare
- Block office (BDO) help desks: pension forms, PM Awas Gramin and Mukhyamantri Awas follow-up, social security
- Panchayat bhawan / Panchayat Sarkar Bhawan: RTPS counter, the mukhiya's office, ward-level verification for schemes
- Anganwadi centres: nutrition, maternity benefit (PMMVY), child registration
- Social security cell / Buniyad Kendra: pensions, disability support (see 2.5)
- Help with scheme eligibility checks and DBT payment failures (bank account seeding, e-KYC)

**Where:** block HQs and larger panchayats in all the rural blocks. In urban wards, Patna Municipal Corporation circle offices (Kankarbagh, Patna City, Bankipur area).

### 2.3 SHGs & Village Organisations (JEEViKA)
JEEViKA (Bihar Rural Livelihoods Promotion Society, BRLPS) works in three tiers: **SHG** (10–12 women, the "didis") → **Village Organisation (VO)**, a federation of 10–15 SHGs → **Cluster Level Federation (CLF)**, a federation of VOs, usually 1–2 per block.
- SHG formation, bookkeeping and the regular-saving habit; community cadres (CMs, bookkeepers, Bank Mitras)
- SHG–bank credit linkage and the Community Investment Fund for loans to members
- Livelihood support: dairy, goatery, poultry, vegetable producer groups, Didi ki Rasoi canteens, producer companies
- Satat Jeevikoparjan Yojana for ultra-poor households (see Part 3)
- Social action: health, nutrition and WASH awareness; insurance enrolment (PMJJBY/PMSBY); campaigns on alcohol and dowry

**Where:** CLFs plausibly sit in each rural block HQ: Naubatpur, Punpun, Masaurhi, Bihta, Maner, Fatuha, Sampatchak, Phulwari, Danapur, Paliganj. JEEViKA's urban arm (and DAY-NULM area-level federations) fits Patna City and Kankarbagh.

### 2.4 Skilling & Jobs
- ITIs: electrician, fitter, welder, COPA (computer) trades
- DDU-GKY residential placement-linked training for rural youth, mobilised through JEEViKA
- PMKVY / Skill India centres: short courses in retail, beauty, healthcare assistance, logistics
- Kushal Yuva Program (KYP) centres: basic computer, spoken English (Hindi), soft skills; often linked to the District Registration and Counselling Centre (DRCC)
- DRCC: registration for Student Credit Card, KYP and Swayam Sahayata Bhatta; job fairs (rozgar mela)

**Where:** the DRCC and ITIs in the city (Boring Road / Bailey Road side, Rajendra Nagar). KYP centres are in nearly every block town (Danapur, Bihta, Masaurhi, Fatuha, Phulwari, Kankarbagh). A DDU-GKY centre plausibly in Bihta or Danapur.

### 2.5 Health & Disability
- PHCs and CHCs at block HQs: OPD, immunisation, maternal care, Ayushman card enrolment
- Health and Wellness Centres (Ayushman Arogya Mandir) at sub-centre level: screening for BP, diabetes and cancer
- District hospital / sadar hospital (Gardanibagh area) and large tertiary hospitals in the city
- UDID camps: disability assessment by a medical board, then a UDID card applied for through [swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in)
- District Disability Rehabilitation Centre and Buniyad Kendra (Bihar's one-stop centre for elderly, widows and persons with disabilities): aids and appliances, physiotherapy, pension help

**Where:** PHCs in all rural blocks. The district hospital and DDRC in central Patna (Gardanibagh / Bailey Road side). UDID camps rotate through block HQs: Masaurhi, Bihta, Fatuha, Danapur.

### 2.6 Banking & Credit
- Bank branches (public-sector and regional rural bank, e.g. Dakshin Bihar Gramin Bank): accounts, KCC, Mudra, PMEGP loans
- Customer Service Points (CSP) / Bank Mitra: cash in/out, Jan Dhan accounts, DBT withdrawal, insurance enrolment
- SHG bank linkage: savings accounts for SHGs, cash credit limits, the role of the JEEViKA Bank Mitra
- Financial literacy camps; India Post Payments Bank doorstep service
- Help with failed DBT payments, Aadhaar seeding and NPCI mapping

**Where:** CSPs are in every panchayat-level village market (Naubatpur, Punpun, Sampatchak, Maner, Paliganj). Branches concentrate on Boring Road, Kankarbagh, Patna City and Danapur.

### 2.7 Business Support
- District Industries Centre (DIC): PMEGP, Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana, PM Vishwakarma verification, MSME advice
- Udyam registration help (free, at [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)); GST and FSSAI registration help for food micro-enterprises
- Market linkage for SHG products: Bihar's GI products such as makhana, Madhubani painting, sattu, Maner laddoo and local pickles; Saras melas, haats, ONDC / GeM onboarding, JEEViKA's retail outlets
- Packaging, branding and FSSAI licensing guidance for food processing
- Industrial areas: Fatuha, Bihta, Patliputra (Patna)

**Where:** the DIC in central Patna. Industrial areas in Fatuha, Bihta and Patna City. SHG market linkage in Patna City (wholesale), Kankarbagh, and the craft melas held in central Patna.

### 2.8 NGOs & Community Support
- Counselling: domestic violence, mental health, de-addiction; family counselling centres
- Awareness: legal rights, menstrual health, nutrition, child marriage, digital fraud
- Documentation camps: help with Aadhaar, ration cards, pensions and the UDID card
- Free legal aid through the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA)
- Disability support groups and youth clubs

**Where:** across all localities. Dense urban areas (Kankarbagh, Patna City, Rajendra Nagar) and the larger rural blocks (Masaurhi, Phulwari, Danapur).

## Caveats

- **The coordinates were not checked against a map service.** Verify every pin on OpenStreetMap before seeding, especially rows marked CHECK.
- **Scheme amounts and windows change every year.** Bihar schemes (Udyami, Laghu Udyami, KYP age band, SJY amounts) were confirmed mostly from secondary sources. Show amounts as "up to" and always link to the official portal.
- **PMEGP's legal extension beyond FY2025-26 was not confirmed** from an official order, although it is clearly operating in 2026-27.
- **PM Vishwakarma** has reached its registration target, so new sign-ups may be paused.
- **Helplines:** only the national numbers and the two Bihar lines above were found on official pages. Re-test before launch; one Bihar department helpline was reported unanswered in 2026.
- **Fictional data rule:** the service descriptions are generic types. Do not use real centre names (e.g. a specific ITI, hospital or CLF name) on dots. The exceptions are real scheme names, government portals and public helplines.
- Pension wording is kept neutral and factual; no political attribution should appear in the app.
