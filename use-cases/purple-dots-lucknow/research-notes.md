# Purple Dots Lucknow: research notes

Researched 12 November 2026. These notes are for realism only. The map's data will be **fictional**: no real organisation names, people, addresses or phone numbers go on a dot. The only real things below are locality names, *kinds* of service, public scheme names and public helplines. Where a real institution is named, it is named only so the fictional dots can mirror the *kind* of place it is; do not copy it onto the map.

## 2. Service types: what they typically offer and where

Each "Where" line is a plausibility judgement, not a list of real places.

### 2.1 Disability certificate and UDID
- The CMO office and district-hospital **medical boards** assess the type and percentage of disability on fixed board days. The result feeds the UDID card on the Swavlamban portal ([swavlambancard.gov.in](https://www.swavlambancard.gov.in/)).
- Online application, status tracking, renewal and download on the same portal; offline forms are taken at district counters and digitised ([DEPwD UDID page](https://depwd.gov.in/en/unique-disability-id-udid/)).
- **Certificate camps** at block CHCs, schools and DDRCs. SIPDA funds states to run them ([DEPwD SIPDA](https://depwd.gov.in/en/sipda/)), and Basti ran one for children in October 2026 ([Amar Ujala, Basti](https://www.amarujala.com/uttar-pradesh/basti/disability-certificates-were-issued-to-21-children-at-the-camp-basti-news-c-207-1-bst1005-169327-2026-10-14)).
- Help desks at the district Divyangjan Sashaktikaran (disability welfare) office for forms, Aadhaar linking and photographs.
- **Where:** Hazratganj and Aminabad side (large government hospitals), Gomti Nagar (big institute hospital), Basti town and Kaili. Camps in Chinhat, Jankipuram, Bahadurpur and Walterganj.

### 2.2 Assistive devices
- **ADIP camps** (assessment camp first, distribution camp later) run by ALIMCO, national institutes, CRCs or NGOs. ALIMCO is headquartered in Kanpur and has a toll-free helpline, 1800-180-5129.
- Items for locomotor disability: tricycles, wheelchairs, crutches, **calipers**, **artificial limbs (prosthetics)**, and motorised tricycles or wheelchairs for severe disability. The UP state scheme lists similar items: tricycles, wheelchairs, hearing aids, white canes, smart canes and crutches ([district welfare page](https://hamirpur.nic.in/hi/handicap-welfare-department/)).
- Items for hearing disability: **hearing aids** (behind-the-ear), and cochlear implants for children.
- Items for visual disability: **smart canes**, low-vision aids, an **accessible mobile phone** for visually impaired students (once in 5 years), and a **laptop, Braille note-taker or Brailler** for students in Class 10 and above (once in 10 years). See part 3, ADIP.
- Prosthetic and orthotic (P&O) workshops for measurement, fitting, gait training and repairs.
- **Where:** a Rajajipuram or Mohan Road P&O workshop; camps at Charbagh-side community halls, Alambagh and Chinhat; a Basti town camp and the Kaili hospital P&O unit.

### 2.3 Rehabilitation and therapy
- The **DDRC** (District Disability Rehabilitation Centre) offers assessment, physiotherapy, speech therapy, counselling, referrals for devices, and help with certificates. UP now funds all 38 of its DDRCs from state resources and approved 18 divisional Divyangjan Sashaktikaran Kendras in December 2025 ([Organiser, July 2026](https://organiser.org/2026/07/04/362826/bharat/seva-samvedna-sammaan-how-yogi-government-rewrote-uttar-pradeshs-compact-with-its-divyangjan/)).
- The **Composite Regional Centre (CRC) Lucknow**, on Mohan Road, is a central-government centre. It offers physiotherapy, occupational therapy, P&O, speech and audiology, special education and vocational guidance.
- **Low-vision rehabilitation:** functional vision assessment, magnifier trials, orientation and mobility (white-cane) training, and **daily-living skills** (cooking, money handling, phone use).
- **Physiotherapy:** hospital physical-medicine and rehabilitation (PMR) departments, private clinics, and home-visit physiotherapists, which matter for the Basti persona.
- Professionals should be **RCI-registered** (Rehabilitation Council of India): audiologists, P&O engineers, special educators and rehabilitation psychologists.
- **Where:** Mohan Road/Rajajipuram, Indira Nagar and Vikas Nagar (private physiotherapy clinics), Gomti Nagar (hospital PMR), Chinhat (outreach). In Basti: the town DDRC, the Kaili physiotherapy department, and home visits to Walterganj and Bahadurpur.

### 2.4 Skilling for PwDs
- The **PM-DAKSH-DEPwD portal** ([pmdaksh.depwd.gov.in](https://pmdaksh.depwd.gov.in)) offers more than 250 courses through empanelled training partners. Registration uses the UDID number. It has a job module called "Divyangjan Rozgar Setu".
- Under **NAP-SDP**, NGO training partners run free courses (often with a stipend) in data entry, retail sales, hospitality, beauty, tailoring and mobile repair ([DEPwD NAP-SDP](https://depwd.gov.in/en/skill-development-of-pwds/)).
- **Computer training with screen readers** (NVDA, TalkBack), typing, MS Office, and email and internet use for blind and low-vision learners.
- Courses for deaf learners taught in Indian Sign Language: tailoring, graphic design and beauty.
- A **National Career Service Centre for Differently Abled** (formerly a VRC) is at Kanpur. It provides free vocational evaluation and courses. Lucknow has no NCSC-DA of its own ([DGE](https://dge.gov.in/dge/sites/default/files/2023-02/Advertimen_for_NCSC-DAs_0.pdf)).
- **Where:** Alambagh, Aliganj and Kapoorthala (private institutes), Mohan Road (university and CRC), Gomti Nagar. In Basti: a town ITI or skill centre.

### 2.5 Jobs and inclusive employers
- **Retail:** supermarket and mall chains in Gomti Nagar, Hazratganj and Alambagh hire for store operations, packing and billing. Deaf and low-vision staff are common hires.
- **BPO, IT and back-office** work in Gomti Nagar (Vibhuti Khand) and Hazratganj: voice and non-voice process jobs, data entry and screen-reader-friendly roles.
- **Hospitality:** hotels and cafés in Hazratganj, Gomti Nagar and Husainabad (heritage tourism) offer kitchen, housekeeping and front-desk work. Some cafés across India are deliberately staffed by deaf workers.
- **Manufacturing and warehousing:** Chinhat, Talkatora, Amausi/Sarojini Nagar and Nadarganj industrial areas offer assembly, packing and quality-check jobs.
- **Placement agencies and job fairs for PwDs.** DEPwD lists Enable India and Youth4Jobs among its employability helplines ([DEPwD other helplines](https://depwd.gov.in/en/others-helplines/)). District employment offices hold *rozgar melas*, and the NCS portal lists jobs.
- **Where:** Gomti Nagar, Hazratganj, Alambagh, Chinhat and Aminabad. Husainabad suits the 28-year-old persona because it is near the old-city retail and tourism jobs.

### 2.6 Schemes and pensions
- The district office of the **Divyangjan Sashaktikaran Vibhag** (UP's Department for Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities) handles the pension (applied for on [sspy-up.gov.in](https://sspy-up.gov.in)), the marriage incentive, the shop scheme, the devices scheme, and the surgery grant ([district welfare page](https://hamirpur.nic.in/hi/handicap-welfare-department/)).
- **Divyang pension** is ₹1,000 a month, paid by DBT to the bank. See part 3 for the details and the central change in October 2026.
- **Shop construction or operation** loan-cum-grant; **marriage incentive** (applied for on divyangjan.upsdc.gov.in); free **UPSRTC** bus travel.
- **NHFDC** self-employment loans go through the state channelising agency.
- Help desks at Common Service Centres (CSCs) and Jan Seva Kendras for online forms.
- **Where:** the district office in Lucknow (central: Hazratganj or Kaiserbagh side); CSCs in every locality; Basti town (Collectorate area).

### 2.7 Education and assistive-tech learning
- **Screen readers:** NVDA (free, Windows) and TalkBack (Android). Training in OCR apps, magnification software, large-print settings and smartphone accessibility.
- **Low-vision devices:** handheld and stand magnifiers, CCTV/video magnifiers, and reading glasses with prisms.
- **Special and integrated schools.** UP runs integrated special secondary schools (Class 6 to 12) in 7 districts including Lucknow. There are 28 state special schools in all ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/up-pioneers-inclusive-education-integrated-schools-for-divyang-students-take-shape-in-7-districts)).
- **Higher education:** a state rehabilitation university on Mohan Road reserves 50% of seats for students with disabilities. It opened UP's first Braille library, with more than 4,000 books, which members of the public can join ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/uttar-pradesh-launches-first-braille-library-dsmnru-lucknow-4000-books-inclusive-education-divyangjan/amp)).
- Braille instruction, Indian Sign Language classes, and B.Ed (Special Education) colleges.
- **Where:** Mohan Road/Rajajipuram, Aliganj (a good fit for the 20-year-old learning assistive tech), Mahanagar and Indira Nagar. In Basti: a town special school or an inclusive-education resource centre at block level.

### 2.8 NGOs and support groups
- Disabled people's organisations (DPOs): **blind associations**, **deaf associations** (with ISL clubs), and **polio survivors' groups** for peer support and help with scheme applications.
- **Parent groups** for children with disabilities. National Trust registered organisations run local parent groups.
- **Women-with-disabilities collectives** for self-help groups and livelihood. These fit the Alambagh and Chinhat personas.
- **Community-based rehabilitation (CBR) workers** in villages. They are key for Basti home visits and caliper follow-up.
- Free legal aid and rights awareness under the RPwD Act, through the District Legal Services Authority.
- **Where:** spread across all localities, with heavier presence in Indira Nagar, Aliganj, Aminabad and Alambagh. In Basti: town, Walterganj and Bahadurpur.

## Caveats

- **Fictional data only.** Real institutions named above (CRC Lucknow, the Mohan Road university, ALIMCO, NCSC-DA Kanpur, the Kaili hospital) are context. The map must use invented names, addresses and numbers. Real helplines from part 4 may be shown as helplines, but not as a dot's own phone number.
- **Coordinates** come from OpenStreetMap centroids. Some (Telibagh, Mohan Road, Basti town and the district hospital, Bahadurpur, Bargadwa) are a point of interest standing in for a centre. Bargadwa is about 26 km north of Basti town, so use Walterganj as the "near Basti town" village.
