# Red Dots Guwahati — Use Case Brief

Source: Red Dots AI infographic. Help exists on the road (hospitals, mechanics, tow operators, schemes) but can't be found the moment it's needed. ~4.95 lakh road incidents recorded in India in 2024. Before: "I have been stranded on the side of the road for hours now." After: "I found a mechanic just minutes after my vehicle broke down."
