# White Dots Lucknow: use case brief

**Source:** the White Dots AI infographic.
- **The problem:** a household has many needs at once. Most of the help is close by, but none of it is visible, so jobs stay unfilled, skills stay unused, and people leave for bigger cities.
- **Before:** "Every time someone at home has a need, we end up asking ten different people."
- **After:** "Turns out many of my family's needs can be met by local services."

White Dots is the umbrella over every other colour. One bot handles a whole family's needs, and one map shows all nine Dot colours in a district.

```
## 1. Programme      White Dots Lucknow
## 2. Colour         Neutral white/charcoal UI (#1F2937 text, #F8FAFC background). Each pin and filter uses its own Dot colour:
                     Blue Jobs #2F6FD6 · Yellow Education & Skilling #C99700 · Green Farm & Rural #2D6A2E ·
                     Pink Women's Jobs & Support #C2185B · Purple Disability Support #7B5EA7 · Red Emergency #DC143C ·
                     Orange Local Experiences #E8731A · Brown Business: Talent, Capital & Suppliers #8B5A2B ·
                     Golden SHGs, Customers & Services #A8790F
## 3. Geography     Lucknow district: city wards plus the rural edge (Malihabad mango belt, Bakshi Ka Talab, Mohanlalganj)
## 4. Seekers        Members of one household, each with a different need; plus requests from other households on the map
## 5. Providers      Whatever each colour covers: employers, colleges and skill centres, farm services, women's groups,
                     disability support, emergency services, experience hosts, suppliers and lenders, SHGs and customers
## 6. Bot name       Parivar Saathi (परिवार साथी), male voice
## 7. Language       Hindi
## 8. Personas       One family in Alambagh: son, daughter, father, mother (personas.md)
## 9. Sectors        The nine Dot colours above. Requests sit inside their colour as white pins with a coloured ring.
## 10. Map "You"     The family's home in Alambagh
## 11. Data          Fictional; real localities and scheme names; national helplines only
```
